P1 — REDBRAND FULL PAINTING DOES NOT PRESERVE THE SOURCE FLOORPLAN
Scene/media ID: redbrand-hideout. Asset: lmop-battle-maps/professional/redbrand-hideout.png. Classification: needs-correction before source-faithful exploration.
The general composition is recognizable: cellar southeast, crypt/pen area northeast, western living rooms, central crevasse and two bridges. Approximately twelve room/cavern footprints are present. This broad resemblance is insufficient for keyed exploration.
1. Source area 8 (pp.23–24) is a dry fissure 5–10 feet wide and 20 feet deep, with rubble at the bottom. The full painting unmistakably renders blue reflective, flowing water. This changes terrain, fall interpretation, visibility and the nothic's hidden body/cache context.
2. The source's forest tunnel continues south from the cavern, about 100 feet beyond the map. The painting ends the southern area in a small walled recess rather than a clear off-map tunnel. The cellar's eastern entry landing and two short stair flights (p.20) are also absent; the painted cellar is a flat rectangle with no usable representation of that entrance.
3. Several important connections are absent or ambiguous: the ordinary 11–12 door is a continuous wall in the painting; the area 1–3 stair/corridor route is replaced by a solid wall/rock gap; the source's 6–4 access and 4–5 access are not legibly reproduced, while a conspicuous boundary opening appears between the upper-right rooms. Secret 12–7 passage/stair geometry and the 7–6 secret connection are not faithfully represented. The southwest secret cellar route is not preserved in its source configuration. These affect access, search choices and Glasstaff's escape (p.25).
4. Functional room contents are substantially lost: area 5 has benches instead of two barred cells with a central guard space (p.22); area 2 is effectively empty instead of two double bunks and supplies (pp.20–21); area 11 lacks the alchemical worktable/apparatus; area 12 lacks the bed, desk and chest (p.25). Area 9 shows two large bedlike objects rather than the keyed four bunks. Three elongated shapes remain in the crypt area but lack the source's distinct sarcophagus placement.
5. The hall corresponding to area 3 contains a conspicuous dark rectangular three-part object/recess. It is not a clear ordinary tiled floor. I cannot identify it conclusively as a revealed pit, but it risks advertising an obstruction or trap where the source specifies a concealed false floor. Correct the normal unrevealed floor; reveal a discovered trap separately.
6. No readable room numbers, named labels, enemy tokens, captives, explicit secret-door S symbols or labeled treasure are baked into this painting. That is a useful player-art property, but it does not cure wrong topology or terrain. Fog is still required for unseen rooms; showing the entire image would reveal all its geometry.
Scale: source p.21 explicitly states one square = 5 feet. The app applies 30 columns to a 1536x1024 image: nominal 150 by 100 feet. The painted masonry/paving is a decorative irregular pattern, not a verified 30x20 five-foot grid, and room/bridge proportions have changed. Do not claim source scale or correct this solely by changing the grid count. Rebuild or reconcile geometry, then calibrate scale with known room spans.
Preservation requirement: retain redbrand-hideout ID and saved scene data. Replacing base art does not justify resetting fog, walls, tokens, room states or passages; changed geometry requires an explicit coordinate/migration review.
