# Settlements & people

Refresh the DM app, then open **World → Towns** for the settlement ledger or **World → Mending Court** for the new home and relationship workspace. The number beside Towns shows developments due for review.

## Between expeditions

Open **World → Between expeditions**, or use the button in **Mending Court** or **Towns**. The workspace has four tabs:

1. **Opportunities:** twelve original proposals across commissions, shopping, research, training, employment, repairs and relationships. Filter by character. Each includes proposed effort, cost, prerequisites, uncertainty, consequences and alternatives. All five characters have opportunities; no Waterdeep event is assumed to have happened.
2. **Work & review:** edit a prepared activity, then choose **Review activity outcome**. Record what actually happened and the actual cost (including “none” or “unknown” when appropriate), choose the result and audience, preview the linked changes, and apply. Link NPC knowledge, relationships, promises, custody or quests through the existing review controls. Partial progress can be corrected on the same record; withdrawing approval restores the pending proposal.
3. **Correspondence:** draft Dianna’s reply, Pebble’s inquiry to his hammer-makers, Trista’s contact request, Aldric’s records inquiry, or a custom message. Replace any bracketed prompts and record whether this is a player copy, an in-world dispatch, or receipt. **Preview letter for this phone** opens the normal outcome review; applying it publishes only the reviewed wording to the chosen character’s private phone journal. A fictional recipient learns nothing automatically, and no external email or message is sent.
4. **Town return:** review approved changes since departure, separately inspect unresolved plans, generate a short safe summary, and deliberately show it on TV or review it as a shared journal entry. Private letters, private results and proposed consequences stay out. Correcting a pre-departure event does not move it into the away-period summary.

**Time and payments:** activity effort is recorded, while **Work & review → Account for shared time** advances one reviewed block for the whole party. Parallel activities can share the same afternoon. This advances exploration durations and settlement deadlines once; it does not apply rests. Use the top-bar Undo for elapsed time, **Undo preparation** for drafts, and Outcome review’s undo/correction tools for approved results. Actual payments, stock and inventory still use the linked character and shop tools; this workflow does not spend currency, grant training benefits or create items automatically.

Existing NPC plans and deadlines now use the same approved outcome record. Due dates propose a review; they never carry out a consequence. Four optional Waterdeep plans can be prepared after checking their prerequisites. Legacy town records remain intact. Use **Advanced town tools** for shop stock, work units, postponement, custom deadlines and other existing settlement controls.

Durations are editable narrative estimates; prices remain unquoted until agreed. Complete rules-based training or crafting procedures need the selected source’s full requirements. Source books are unchanged. The implementation was verified with an isolated practice campaign, including actual browser navigation, clock handling, save/reload, correction/undo and production TV/phone privacy checks. Physical devices still use the normal table check.

## Mending Court · home and relationships

The courtyard is future preparation. No newer play has been confirmed beyond the Redbrand-hideout stopping point. Its proposed location is within walking distance of the Yawning Portal; no exact street or ward has been imposed.

**Home & lodging** has a clickable seven-space schematic: street passage, available rooms, bindery, well/washing space, covered table/repair bench, pantry and side stair. The plan is illustrative, not a measured battle grid. Preview it privately, then explicitly choose **Show on TV**. A session must contain the courtyard scene before showing it; **Returns & scenes → Open or create home session** creates or reuses the recurring home session and switches the table.

Discuss ten negotiable terms: use of rooms, occupants, rent, deposit, keys/access, storage, shared spaces, repairs, messages and notice. Nessa is a proposed resident leaseholder able to sublet; Dracuseth lives nearby and provides introductions. Record only terms actually accepted. Trial lodging, an address for messages and staying elsewhere are valid choices. No money, time, property ownership or inventory changes automatically.

**People & promises** separates:

- Proposed personality, wants, routines and relationships with other residents.
- Approved wants, last interaction, attitude, intentions and whereabouts.
- Knowledge assigned through each outcome's **Who knows** choices.
- Explicitly linked promises, including open and resolved results.

Nessa and Orenna are the first introductions. Jori, their adult brother Pellan, and Aven appear in later ordinary scenes. Dracuseth remains an active city contact with a life and commitments of his own. Every character has optional ways to contribute, rest, maintain privacy or decline.

Use **Record an interaction**, **Record what they learned**, or **Record a promise**. These open the existing outcome review. Write the actual event, choose only the fields that changed, preview the linked effects, and apply. Blank updated fields explicitly clear earlier values; untouched fields preserve them. Knowledge is not inferred from involvement. Corrections, withdrawal, undo and redo update the relationship card and subsequent scene guidance together. Existing Story/Settlement notes are retained as preparation or legacy context.

**Returns & scenes** contains eleven beats: arrival, terms, unpacking, three ordinary returns, supper, quiet study, Dracuseth's visit, an improvement, and an optional later disturbance. The “Next useful scene” follows confirmed history. Recording a scene is separate from selecting or staging it. Use one return key for several scenes during the same expedition return so it counts once. Disturbances require three distinct approved ordinary returns and a deliberate enable action; they never trigger from a clock or loading the app.

Approved changes to relationships, promises, lodging and spaces appear in private future-scene guidance. Player narration remains deliberately reviewed. To share a journal entry, use **Publish wording to** in Outcome review, select the proper knowers/recipients and review the exact player-safe wording. Raw NPC records, room contents, rent notes and private character disclosures stay off TV and phone data. Quest routing and Active/Completed views remain unchanged.

The home pack carries current HP, character statistics and resources. It creates no foes, awards no levels, heals nobody and completes no quests. Reuse it for later returns. Music cues remain manually controlled; the ordinary home is established before any optional mystery.

Isolated browser and production-data rehearsals cover the plan, approval workflow, TV preview/show, distinct-return gate, character preservation and save/reload. Physical devices and speaker output need the usual table check.

## Town return

Review changes since departure, outstanding deadlines, and approved campaign events. Travel marker changes automatically record departure/return for matching settlements (including Phandalin); use the visit button when narrating travel without the journey map.

**Draft return narration** uses confirmed Phandalin variants and outcomes you explicitly marked shareable. Edit it, then choose **Show reviewed return text on TV**. Private plans and NPC notes stay on the DM screen. The normal handout controls clear the text afterward.

## Town state

Phandalin has separate Redbrand, family-rescue, and mine-future states. They begin unconfirmed. Each change requires evidence or a table ruling. These states change the return narration; they do not silently complete quests, rescue prisoners, distribute rewards, or rewrite continuity events.

Add other settlements here. Their return summaries use the developments you record; Phandalin’s three narrative variants are campaign-specific.

## NPCs

Existing campaign people show their assigned continuity knowledge, wants, last interaction, next move, and whereabouts. Rumors remain labeled. Extra knowledge requires your notes; NPCs do not automatically learn everything the party knows.

Current settlement notes are also visible from the existing Story NPC card. Original reference details remain intact. A continuing plan can be prepared from an NPC’s card.

## Shops & services

Add providers, stock, services, prices, requirements, and availability. Unknown quantities stay unknown until confirmed. Preview stock changes before applying them. Prepare item orders or commissions with deadlines and proposed delivery outcomes.

This is a local shop ledger: record purchases, payments, and transfers in character/party inventory separately. No prices, starting stock, or resource deductions are invented.

## Plans & downtime

Prepare ally/enemy plans, faction deadlines, orders, commissions, or projects for research, training, crafting, employment, and relationship repair. Record costs, participants, and prerequisites. Work units are defined by the DM; finishing them makes a project ready for review.

Optional campaign plan prompts cover Yeemik’s bargain, Sildar’s investigation, Kost’s disappearance, returning families, and the mine’s stakeholders. These are custom suggestions, not established events or book rules.

Due developments never apply automatically. Review the actual result, then approve, decline, or postpone it. Mark a result shareable only if it belongs in the players’ return recap.

The settlement clock starts at day 1 when tracking begins. It counts subsequent travel/exploration/downtime minutes and persists across sessions. It is a relative clock, separate from the Story calendar’s date labels. Advance downtime here to tick exploration timers too; this does not apply rests or pay project costs. Time advancement is blocked during combat.

## Reliability

Records are saved with campaign extras and included in existing backup and Undo/Redo flows. The TV and phones never receive the private ledger. Automated model, control, and production integration checks cover save roundtrips, travel timing, deadline review, stock previews, return boundaries, Undo/Redo, and player privacy. The Mending Court workspace also passed an isolated browser rehearsal, including TV preview and save/reload. The physical table check remains separate.
