## Waterdeep scenes

### Waterdeep arrival

> The road gives way to streets that seem to have no end. Bells sound somewhere above the rooftops; below them, carts, voices and footsteps press onward. For the first time in days, the question is not where you can go, but where you want to belong.

Ask each player one thing they seek in the city. Confirm the actual Phandalin ending, Gundren/Nundro, promises and artifact custody; do not award Echo’s Whisper unless play did. Offer lodging, research, paid work or the well; none compulsory.

Durnan offers a return contact; Dracuseth has arranged a table only if contacted. No faction enrollment.

### Dracuseth at the Portal

Use this meeting only after contact has actually been arranged. If he is occupied or has not received a message, Durnan can take one; the party can pursue another lead and return. The transition's `tr-portal` / `tr-dracuseth` beats provide that branch. Do not replay this introduction if it already happened in the transition session.

> Dracuseth rises from a table near the wall. “There you are. Sit down. Tell me what happened before anyone tells you what it means.”

He remains based in Waterdeep and is not a sixth party member. He can keep a sealed message, arrange a meeting with Lantern House, escort a threatened city witness, or hold supplies by explicit agreement. He knows only what he has actually been told. He cannot solve the Nine or command a rescue in the dungeon.

Record one practical promise and who can call on him. Follow the existing NPC card, not a prewritten reunion outcome.

### The Yawning Portal

> Conversation rolls around the great well like water around a rock. A rope creaks. Someone at the next table stops talking to listen.

Durnan asks who should be contacted if the party is late. Kestrel’s crew offers a competitive introduction without mandatory hostility. Ringgo can negotiate legitimate work, Trista chooses what to disclose, Pebble chooses how to introduce himself, Donavan can ask about logs, Aldric can decline attention. Book pp6–9; verify fees at p7 before charging.

Two leads maximum: Second Margin for evidence, or a bounded first descent. Music suggestion: low tavern ambience; leave dialogue audible.

### Second Margin research visit

> Three trays sit on the desk: observations, interpretations, and unanswered questions. Selise places a clean sheet in front of you. “Start with what someone else could check.”

Selise asks permission before copying any object. Compare actual notes from Phandalin; no object is assumed present. Donavan chooses a redacted research question. Pebble compares mineral samples visually or with tools; no eating an unknown magical sample is required. Aldric can ask about a signet or dates without disclosing trauma.

Produce a bounded question, a source, and a follow-up meeting; no instant cosmology answer. The Silent Library stays unrevealed until its missing-stair investigation.

### Ninepin Works: safe comparison

> Ceramic dishes sit between brass instruments. Orren turns a tool around so you can see its edge. “Nothing cut, crushed, or heated unless you agree.”

Offer Pebble a commission to compare ordinary local stone with a sample he chooses. Pay/terms agreed before work. Aldric may request a non-destructive signet inspection; match an observable maker mark to a record later, not a complete recovered memory.

A written receipt records ownership, tests allowed, sample returned, payment and delivery date. Use Towns to track the commission.

### Offers with strings attached

> A folded note rests beside your cup. It asks for a conversation, and makes no claim that you owe one.

Choose one: Ilvara requests a redacted account with witness consent; Kestrel offers to exchange mapped routes with credit; Anja requests help keeping civilians clear of a risky experiment; Vess offers expedition logistics with a return plan. All are custom local opportunities. Declining preserves other routes.

Record terms, who knows what, benefit, cost, refusal, and deadline. No hidden automatic membership or irreversible betrayal.

### First descent briefing

> A blank route sheet lies beside a coil of rope. Durnan taps the empty line at the bottom. “That is for the way back.”

Recommended floor1 baseline: four level5 characters; party of five does not erase lethal hazards. Check actual sheets, light, supplies, spells, return money, rally point and abort conditions. Review dungeon travel/magic limits pp10–12. All41 room cards are a reference, not a required route. Prepare next reachable rooms from marked map p15.

Example first outing: entrywell, chosen approach, one contact or threat, then return with a map. Stop sooner when resources or player choices call for it.

### Waterdeep debrief

> The noise of the city returns before the familiar doorway comes into view. The empty line on your route sheet is no longer empty.

Record survivors, moved enemies, spent treasure, rooms, time, rescue commitments and actual discoveries. Suggest settlement consequences for approval; do not reset the floor. Dracuseth listens or acts on an agreed city task, never claims to have followed unseen.

Ask every hero for one observation and one question. Offer rest, downtime, another descent, or a site lead.

## Level 1 room cards

Key facts paraphrased from the supplied Dungeon of the Mad Mage, pp.14–26. Narration is original. Exact creature rules remain in the linked local books.

### 1. Entrywell · DotMM p.14

> Sand covers the floor below the well; battered shields line the walls. A passage leads away into darkness.

A bandit listens through the northern one-way secret door and warns areas 6, 8 and 9. DC 20 Perception hears retreating footsteps. Search sand for mundane castoffs and a PHB trinket. Shields crumble; a concealed Elvish message hints at gates.

Record whether the lookout escaped. The well is the known return route.

Creature references: Bandit — mm p.343

### 2. Hall of Many Pillars · DotMM p.14

> Demon reliefs flank the corridor. Beyond shallow steps, pillars break your view of the room.

2a: concealed doors behind nalfeshnee and dretch reliefs lead to 3 and 4. 2b: two bugbears each host an intellect devourer; Detect Sentience prevents surprise. They retreat south to warn 23 rather than hold position. A snake skeleton is harmless but noisy; the pillar cache is empty.

Move surviving scouts to 23; do not duplicate them. Host death releases its devourer.

Creature references: Bugbear — mm p.33; Intellect Devourer — mm p.191

### 3. Slanted Room · DotMM p.16

> Foul water deepens across a sloping floor. A dim violet glow outlines a damaged fishlike statue.

Psychic gray ooze lurks north of the statue, invisible underwater; use its Psychic Crush variant. Water deepens westward to 6 ft and is unfit to drink. The statue’s harmless aura, removable hollow head, wax and broken arm explain its appearance.

No treasure shortcut or new Echo clue is established by the statue.

Creature references: Psychic Gray Ooze — mm p.243

### 4. With Sword in Hand · DotMM p.16

> A faintly shining sword stands in a wooden support. Small bones lie nearby, and distant voices drift through overhead vents.

The sword radiates dim light 10 ft but is cursed: its wielder cannot discard it; it returns if pried away. Remove curse or similar magic ends the curse. The vents carry unintelligible Waterdeep voices. Tiny or gaseous creatures can escape through the opened vents; this is not a normal party exit.

Telegraph the skeletal hand. Do not impose the curse until someone actually takes the sword.

Creature references: No new creature required.

### 5. Grell Hideout · DotMM p.16

> Blackened pillars of mortared bone divide the room. Deep alcoves open above scattered remains.

Two grells hide in separate north/east alcoves. Contest Perception against their Stealth; cover matters. They pursue prey. No treasure.

Offer retreat and describe reachable passages before rolling initiative.

Creature references: Grell — mm p.172

### 6. Undertakers’ Watch Post · DotMM p.17

> Three stone nobles watch over broken bones and a snapped staff. Light and voices come from adjoining chambers.

6a–d: Uktarl (bandit captain, Performance +4), six bandits, two doppelgangers; add the area-1 lookout only if it arrived. They demand 10 gp per hero using vampire costumes; DC 14 Insight exposes the act. Plates at 6b alert the rooms. Uktarl flees to 7 when hurt; others may seek 8. Repairing the staff causes one loud cry then its magic ends. DC 13 Perception at the dwarf fresco finds the stone key to 14b.

Treasure: 220 cp, 91 sp, 85 gp, silver ring 25 gp, thieves’ tools, explorer’s pack; individual bandits carry 1d6 gp. Track relocations and spent tolls.

Creature references: Bandit Captain — mm p.344; Bandit — mm p.343; Doppelganger — mm p.82

### 7. Vampire Haven · DotMM p.17

> Faded frescoes show people fleeing a vast bat. Dust and abandoned bedding fill the passage.

Secret retreat for Undertakers. Uktarl and Harria may make their final stand here if forced back; do not respawn either. A coffin in 7b has old grave dirt and a vial of holy water, but no vampire.

The fake-vampire gang and the old real crypt are separate facts.

Creature references: Bandit Captain — mm p.344

### 8. Bandit Headquarters · DotMM p.18

> Wyvern remains and crystal fragments lie below a high bone throne. Nearby rooms glow with lantern light.

8a throne: sitting or opening its empty seat triggers two snake attacks, each +8, 1d4+1 piercing and DC 13 Con save for 7d6 poison (half on success). 8b Harria (bandit captain, Performance +4) commands a flesh golem but cannot control berserk behavior. 8c five bandits and a doppelganger wear vampire disguises. Harria retreats to 7; other survivors seek 6.

Supplies: costumes/disguise kits, three packs with 11 days of rations each; bandits and doppelganger carry 1d6 gp apiece, the latter a 10 gp tiger eye. Preserve any survivors.

Creature references: Bandit Captain — mm p.344; Flesh Golem — mm p.169; Bandit — mm p.343; Doppelganger — mm p.82
