## What happened

- The five PCs completed Cragmaw Hideout. They killed Klarg and made a bargain with Yeemik: if they killed Klarg, Yeemik would release Sildar. Yeemik honored the agreement and was left in charge of the hideout.
- They rescued Sildar. He traveled with them to Phandalin.
- The party learned only that **humanoid figures, rather than goblins, took Gundren**. They did **not** learn his destination.
- **DM-confirmed after Session 1:** those abductors were Redbrands, and they took Gundren to Cragmaw Castle. The party has not identified the Redbrands or learned the destination yet.
- The group recovered copper and silver; exact amounts and how they were divided were not recorded here.
- Trista found the dark **BELOW** token and kept it hidden. Donavan noticed her conceal something, but has not been told what she found. Do not put the token in the public party journal yet.
- The strange second pressure after the cave's water rush was described. No player reacted to it at the table.
- Aldric fell to 0 HP. The party used a healing potion found in the hideout to revive him; that potion is spent.
- The whole party completed a long rest. They traveled to Phandalin and stopped **outside Barthen's Provisions, before entering or speaking to anyone there**. Delivery, payment, town conversations, and the next lead have not happened yet.
- All five characters earned level 2. Their players are still choosing HP methods and other level 2 details; do not write final HP totals or wizard spell choices into the live campaign record yet.
