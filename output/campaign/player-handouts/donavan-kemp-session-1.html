The Echo Below · Session 1

Donavan Kemp

Human Wizard · Level 1 · The field researcher

What you know

You come from the Kemp Archive and Arcane University. You want to record what can be proved, especially when someone powerful would rather hide it. Gundren asks you for an honest account of anything unusual at the mine. Your barn owl familiar and your notes may notice different pieces of the same story.

AC12
HP9
Speed30 ft.
Spell DC14

What you can do

  • Spell attack: +6. You have two 1st level spell slots; casting a 1st level spell normally spends one.
  • Cantrips on your sheet: Dancing Lights, Prestidigitation, and Mind Sliver. Mind Sliver forces an Intelligence save and can weaken the target’s next save.
  • Your spellbook lists Mage Armor, Shield, Magic Missile, Catapult, Burning Hands, Detect Magic, Comprehend Languages, Alarm, and Find Familiar. Your prepared selection needs to be confirmed before play.

Keep in mind

  • You are trained in Arcana, History, Investigation, and Religion (+6 each). Your owl can help you observe, but tell the DM how you use it.
  • Arcane Recovery can restore one expended 1st level slot after a short rest, once per day. Mind Sliver and Catapult are approved character exceptions.
