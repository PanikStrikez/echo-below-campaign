The dice roller adds the entered modifier to independent die results. Damage
buttons apply temporary HP before normal HP, and healing caps at entered
maximum HP. They do not decide whether an attack hits or automatically apply
cover, advantage, critical hits, resistance, vulnerability, saves, concentration,
or spell effects. The DM resolves those using the linked 2014 rule or named
character exception. Exhaustion is tracked as a 0–6 level; surprised and other
statuses are recorded manually. Rest recovery remains a manual character-sheet
decision. Initiative ties can be reordered by the DM, as required by the
table's choice rather than by a hidden bonus.

The rules library contains paraphrased 2014 table references for action economy,
surprise, rests, spellcasting, concentration, death saves, and conditions. Each
reference links to a source page and carries a confidence label. Corrections
appear next to the original source without overwriting it.
