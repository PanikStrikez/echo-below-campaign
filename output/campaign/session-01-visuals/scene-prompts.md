# Scene illustration prompts

These prompts document the original image generation. The departure illustration was later revised for the five-PC party; the current art shows Gundren and Sildar without Dracuseth or depicted player characters. These are campaign mood images, not keyed encounter maps.

## 1. Gundren departs

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: session-one Dungeons & Dragons scene handout, opening road scene
Primary request: the moment Gundren and Sildar ride ahead of the supply wagon on the road to Phandalin
Scene/backdrop: damp woodland road after dawn rain, distant hills, the loaded supply wagon partly visible in foreground
Current subject: Gundren Rockseeker, a sturdy dwarf with a weathered auburn beard, mounted and turning back to speak one last time; Sildar, an older human warrior on horseback, slightly farther ahead. Do not depict or define the five player characters.
Style/medium: painterly watercolor and gouache fantasy illustration with subtle ink edges and parchment grain, consistent with The Echo Below campaign art
Composition/framing: wide cinematic landscape, characters readable but environment prominent, no map or grid
Lighting/mood: warm morning gold breaking through rain-dark slate clouds; hopeful travel with a slight undertone of uncertainty
Constraints: no text, labels, logos, or watermark; no monsters, magic effects, or foreshadowing symbols
```

## 2. Empty horses

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: session-one Dungeons & Dragons scene handout, goblin ambush reveal
Primary request: the supply party finds Gundren and Sildar's two abandoned horses on a lonely woodland road
Scene/backdrop: rain-dark forest road near Phandalin, bend in the trail, thick brush and trees on both sides
Subject: two fallen saddled horses lying across the road, travel tack askew, a few arrows lodged nearby; the road ahead disappears into uncertain woodland. No people or goblins visible.
Style/medium: atmospheric hand-painted watercolor and gouache fantasy illustration with subtle ink edges and parchment grain, matched to The Echo Below campaign art
Composition/framing: wide landscape view from the approaching wagon's perspective, the horses clear in midground, woods providing suspenseful cover
Lighting/mood: overcast morning after rain, tense and quiet, compassionate rather than gory
Color palette: rain-dark slate, forest green, brown leather, muted amber light
Constraints: no text, labels, maps, grids, logos, or watermark; no visible monsters; no excessive blood or gore; do not reveal any ambusher locations
```

## 3. Cave mouth

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: session-one Dungeons & Dragons scene handout, Cragmaw Hideout approach
Primary request: the adventurers discover a narrow cave mouth where a stream flows out beneath a wooded hillside
Scene/backdrop: damp forest ravine near Phandalin, mossy rock face, shallow stream rushing over pale stones, faint footpath approaching through wet trees
Subject: a dark but physically ordinary cave entrance, stream emerging from it; a few trampled footprints and broken branches suggest recent travel without showing who made them
Style/medium: hand-painted watercolor and gouache fantasy environment illustration with subtle ink edges and parchment grain, matching The Echo Below campaign visual style
Composition/framing: wide establishing view from the party's approach, cave entrance clearly readable but interior not revealed
Lighting/mood: cool overcast daylight with modest amber reflected light, tense curiosity and real-world danger
Color palette: forest green, wet stone gray, slate blue water, cream highlights
Constraints: no characters, monsters, text, labels, grids, logos, or watermark; no magical portals, glowing runes, obvious Echo imagery, or revealed cave interior
```
