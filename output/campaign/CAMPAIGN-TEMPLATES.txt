CAMPAIGN TEMPLATES AND PORTABLE COPIES

Your Echo Below campaign is unchanged by creating a blank campaign.
Settings > Campaign templates > Create blank campaign starts with one black screen,
no characters, NPCs, discoveries, quests, personal exceptions, or media assignments.
Add scenes in Session > Session editor; add characters in Party; add objectives in
Quests > Add a quest. Review player wording before publishing. One main quest goes
to the TV, side quests to shared phones, and character quests to their owner.
Active and Completed player previews remain in Quests.

Download blank example produces an ordinary campaign backup. Restore it through
Settings > Recovery & campaigns > Backup. To reuse YOUR prepared content, use
Recovery & campaigns > Campaigns > Duplicate. Review private history and character
assignments before using a duplicate for another group. A duplicate is not blank.
Campaign switching closes phone joins to avoid carrying access into another group.

Campaign-Module-Manifest.js identifies reusable tools and the optional Echo Below
modules while retaining their tested dependency order. Campaign-Templates.js owns
the blank profile. Saved profile fields prevent Echo party/map/story/media defaults
from repopulating blank records. The existing prepared Echo tools remain available
in the collapsed Echo Below story packages group. Some legacy modules still mix
engine and source-reference data; this is not a wholesale engine rewrite.

PORTABLE PACKAGE
Extract the ZIP, then run Start-Campaign.py using Python 3.10 or newer.
On Mac, Start-Campaign.command is also supplied. A desktop browser is required.
Restore campaign-backup.json as a NEW campaign. Do not run two table servers on the
same ports. Start phone access and scan its new QR code. Saves remain in that
browser's storage; exporting again is needed to carry later play to another device.

The package contains private DM material. Give players the phone join page, not
this archive. Personal source books are excluded. PERSONAL-BOOKS.json lists their
optional separate destinations. Copy only the books you choose into sources/ on
your own computer. The original synced sources are never changed by packaging.
The included SRD retains its attribution. Maps and audio keep their source notes.

The original exported campaign is retained separately from the normalized package
backup. Restore preserves older character values instead of automatically applying
later level-up migrations. Existing default fields may be added to old schemas.
Known obsolete storeroom catalog references are replaced by the existing correctly
named Redbrand and Cragmaw Castle assets. No play outcomes are invented.
