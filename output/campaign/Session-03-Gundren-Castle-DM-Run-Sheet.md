Ask what evidence they trust, who travels, marching order, light, supplies, and whether Sildar has suitable gear. Let the players choose the route. A castle lead can take them straight into the Neverwinter Wood. Without one, use one of these prepared branches:

## Castle arrival

**Say:** “The trees give way to a low rise. Seven broken towers crowd around a ruined stronghold. The place looks abandoned until you catch a small movement behind a narrow slit in the stone.”

Ask how long they watch, which entrance they use, whether they disguise themselves as Redbrand envoys, and what light they carry. The broken main gate leads to the sentries and trap; the south postern is locked; the north canvas entrance can be found by searching. The app's castle TV map is **unmarked tactical art**, not the exact published floor plan. Keep the [marked castle map](../../sources/pdfcoffee.com_lost-mine-of-phandelverpdf-pdf-free.pdf#page=37) private for walls, doors, and measurements.

Track the castle alarm as **quiet / suspicious / raised**. Sentries can call area 4 goblins and area 6 hobgoblins toward the trap hall. The rubble collapse alerts areas 3, 7, 8, and 9. A guard from area 12 warns Grol and returns two rounds later if able. Move existing creatures between rooms; do not create duplicates.

## If the group reaches Gundren

**Say when they open area 14:** “Voices cut through the door: a harsh demand, then a smooth reply. Inside, furs cover the floor. A battered dwarf lies still near a table while a towering bugbear argues with a dark-clad visitor.”

The printed adventure has Grol bargaining over Gundren and his map. **This campaign's actual play moved Gundren to an inn.** Do not stage him as a hostage or at 0 HP in area 14. Grol and Vyerith can bargain only over information or possessions they actually have. If Grol falls, Vyerith may flee toward area 11; track any item it carries. The printed sack holds **220 sp, 160 ep, and three healing potions**; verify the map's custody before placing it. Gundren's printed offer of **25 gp each** and a **10% share** of a working mine applies only if he actually makes it under suitable circumstances. [Adventure pp. 40–41](../../sources/pdfcoffee.com_lost-mine-of-phandelverpdf-pdf-free.pdf#page=40).
