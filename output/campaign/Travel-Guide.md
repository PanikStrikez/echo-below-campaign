# Journey & travel

Refresh the DM app and Player TV after this update. On phones, refresh the player companion.

## Before traveling

1. Open **Travel** beside **Journey map**.
2. Set the party’s named starting location. The initial position is Phandalin, based on the latest recap. Correct it if needed.
3. Add connected route legs. Open **Route distances & pace settings** to enter missing distances and their source or your own estimate.
4. Choose a pace, then **Preview travel progress** and apply it when ready. Each advance stops at the next destination so you can handle encounters and arrivals.

The route painting and drawn lines are approximate. They never determine miles. The editable adventure preset uses 24 miles in a ten-hour travel day; slow/fast speeds are planning estimates. Camp time is additional. Only the existing five-mile ambush–hideout and fifteen-mile Phandalin–Wave Echo distances are prefilled. Unknown distances prevent advancing that leg.

On the regional map: gold dashes are planned routes, teal lines are traveled routes, and the flag marks the party. Drag the flag on the DM map for a correction; dropping near a known location snaps to it. This passes no time and clears an unstarted plan. Finish or stop active travel before manually moving the flag. A free position needs a named starting location before another route can be planned.

## Discovery & access

Use **Places & arrival**:

- **Hidden:** DM only.
- **Rumored:** phones can list its name, but no map pin or destination request.
- **Located:** known map position; can be requested and planned.
- **Visited:** the party has arrived.
- **Cleared:** a DM-recorded outcome, never automatic.
- **Inaccessible:** independent of discovery; keeps its history and blocks travel to it.

The starting records follow the approved recap: Cragmaw Hideout is visited, with Yeemik still in charge; Wyvern Tor is cleared; Old Owl Well is visited. Cragmaw Castle and Wave Echo Cave remain hidden. No past routes have been invented. Update discovery here as play proceeds; ordinary campaign notes do not silently reveal destinations.

## Arrival

Each location has editable narration, an arrival-map choice, and optional ambience. **Stage arrival preview** shows these to the DM. **Show destination on TV & play ambience** is a separate action. Check the selected map and its existing fog first. Arrival does not deploy enemies, reveal fog, or advance combat. The narration remains on the DM card to read aloud. A destination without an available map needs a map selected in that session.

## Camp, watches & events

- Record pooled ration-days and gallons of water; blank means untracked. This ledger is separate from character equipment.
- Enter camp hours, watch order/times, and the actual supply costs you intend to apply.
- Preview before applying. Untracked costs or shortages require an explicit manual ruling, such as foraging or refilling water. Stock never becomes negative.
- Camp advances the exploration clock and timers. **Open character rest review** separately reviews HP, Hit Dice and resources.
- Choose clear, rain or mist to update the scene weather. Arrival ambience is selected separately.
- Write travel events privately or share them to phones. Resolve encounters and extra delays manually. Existing wandering-check and timer reminders still work.

## Player requests

On a phone: **Table → Journey & destination requests**. Choose a located, accessible destination, add a reason and ask the DM. Waiting requests can be revised or cancelled.

The DM’s **Travel** button shows the waiting count. Approval stages a connected route for review; it does not move the party, pass time, or display the destination. Each phone receives only its own request notes and statuses.

## Recovery

Travel records are stored with the campaign and included in existing backups. **Undo/Redo** restores travel, supply costs and exploration time together. Travel and camp advancement are blocked while combat is active.

## Verification

Automated checks cover route progress and estimates, undefined distances, stale reviews, supply shortages, persistence, Undo/Redo, hidden locations and rumors, arrival preview separation, drag behavior, phone ownership/revisions/cancellation, and offline disabling. Test your real TV and phones before relying on it at the table; physical-device playback and layout were not exercised by these automated checks.
