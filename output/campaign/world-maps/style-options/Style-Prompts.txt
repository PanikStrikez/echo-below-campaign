01 — REALISTIC BIRD'S-EYE

Create one finished landscape 3:2 top-down map image for THE ECHO BELOW D&D campaign, one of five visual style studies. The images should share comparable map extent, geography, label set and campaign-site placements so the user can choose an art style.

Correct relationships: Mount Hotenow northeast of Neverwinter; Longsaddle north of Triboar; Yartar east of Triboar; Silverymoon north of Everlund; Phandalin inland east of Leilon/southeast of Neverwinter; Waterdeep south of the Mere of Dead Men; Daggerford south of Waterdeep; Loudwater east/inland of Daggerford, southwest of Greypeaks; Baldur's Gate much farther south; Candlekeep south of Baldur's Gate on coast. A few coherent roads follow reference High Road, Long Road and Trade Way; rivers run naturally.
Include ALL NINE distinctive but small campaign-site glyphs with Roman numerals and SHORT readable names placed physically on map: I Undermountain and III Silent Library together at Waterdeep with separate leader lines, II Wave Echo Cave east/northeast of Phandalin; IV Drowned Observatory offshore west of Leilon; V Dead God's Scar in a valley on western edge of High Moor east of Daggerford; VI Frozen Door in the Reghed Glacier area northeast of Icewind Dale; VII Walking Tower in western Anauroch with a faint dotted roaming oval; VIII Broken Star in Star Mounts, star/rift icon and tiny 'planar reach'; IX Hollow Mountain in southern Greypeaks northeast of Loudwater. These placements are custom campaign proposals, not discovered destinations. Do not assign a god, ancestry, NPC family, or story outcome.
Keep the paired Waterdeep sites legible without creating a giant close-up or blocking coastline. Use small city/settlement symbols and unique site silhouettes. No glowing network between sites, no hex grid, no encounter statistics, no copied logos/signature.

PHOTOREALISTIC-NATURAL ART DIRECTION: convincingly real terrain seen vertically from extreme altitude, cinematic physically plausible miniature world, like a finely detailed aerial terrain scan blended with premium realistic tabletop battle-map art. Natural emerald and olive tree canopies; individual crags and eroded limestone; snowy ridges in the north; deep navy and turquoise sea with shallow-water bathymetry; believable rivers, farm-field mosaics and very tiny overhead roof plans for towns. Gentle clear daylight with geographic relief shadows, no clouds hiding important land, no perspective tilt. NOT an antique illustrated atlas, NOT an ink drawing, NO parchment anywhere, NO gold border, NO side-elevation cartoon mountains, NO oversized castles. Restrained white/ivory cartographic labels with thin dark halos, small muted-brass settlement dots and subdued violet site symbols. Terrain has photographic texture and natural irregularity. Every mountain reads as top-down relief. Highest visual realism of the five.
Comparison label (exact): '01 — REALISTIC BIRD'S-EYE'.
