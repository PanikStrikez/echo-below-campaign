# Character management

Open **Party / Characters** from the DM menu and choose a character.

## Level up

1. Open **Level up**. Choose the advancing class’s Hit Die and update the class description, including each class level for multiclass characters.
2. Enter a physical HP roll, choose the fixed amount, or roll in the app. The app adds Constitution and adjusts prior levels if Constitution changes. Current damage is preserved unless you select the full-heal option.
3. Review ability choices, AC, speed, skill/save overrides, features, spells, attacks and resource maxima. Expand each group to edit it or add an entry. Record source pages and choices.
4. Complete the four review checks, then **Preview level-up**. Open the comparison groups and apply when ready.

Level, proficiency, HP and the new Hit Die are calculated. Class/subclass features, feats, spell choices, explicit attack modifiers, save DCs and slot progression are reviewed choices; the app does not choose them for a player. The current record is saved in history before the update. A live-sheet change invalidates the preview.

## Replace a sheet

**Import sheet** accepts this app’s exported character JSON. It now opens **Import review** instead of replacing the sheet immediately. Compare the incoming record, choose groups, and preview the selected changes. By default, current status and compatible resource spending are retained. The character keeps its table identity and phone assignment. The previous record is available in history; session Undo also restores the change.

PDFs and third-party character exports need conversion into the app’s character record first. This importer does not extract them automatically.

## Cast

**Cast** lets you choose a spell, slot or feature resource, targets, concentration, and reviewed upcast dice/effects. Continue to **DM action review** for attack/save rolls, resource changes and effects. Nothing is spent until that review is applied.

Phones can choose an available spell slot or request ritual/free casting. Upcast effects that change target counts, missiles, damage components or duration still need explicit review. In the review panel, add the necessary resolution steps and check each target. A ritual or free/feature casting requires a ruling note. Casting is recorded and undoable through the existing action history.

## Favorites

Use **Favorites** to pin each character’s common attacks, spells and abilities. Their phone shows these first. Favorites stay with the character in saves and exports.

## Group rest

1. Open **Group rest** and select participating characters. PCs are selected initially; allies can be included when their rest statistics are known.
2. Choose short or long rest. For a short rest, enter each player’s Hit Dice choice, then roll those dice or enter their healing total including Constitution per die.
3. For multiclass characters, confirm each die pool if older data only recorded the total. For a long rest, choose which spent dice recover. The review uses the character’s stored 2014/2024 profile.
4. Use exceptional recovery for features such as Arcane Recovery or story restrictions. Set the resulting slot counts and remaining feature use, with a source/ruling note. Normal recovery can be disabled per character.
5. Preview everyone’s changes, then apply the group once. No part is applied if a reviewed character changed in the meantime.

The DM still checks rest duration, interruptions and eligibility. Choices and rolls in the draft do not spend resources before application. Starting a fresh review clears its draft.

## Phones and handouts

The phone sheet updates from the same live character record as the DM sheet. **Print sheet** creates the full current handout; choose **Save as PDF** in the print dialog. **Save offline sheet** on a phone uses the same handout renderer and can also be printed. Downloaded/printed copies are dated snapshots; live phone sheets continue updating.

The shared handout includes abilities, saves, skills, attacks, spell notes, resources, Hit Dice, equipment, features and public notes. Private DM notes and level-history snapshots are excluded.
