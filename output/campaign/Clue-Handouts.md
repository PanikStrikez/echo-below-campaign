# The Echo Below — Six Recovered Documents

**Prepared future material:** these handouts and their recovery scenes are planned, not proof that players found them. Publish each only after the actual discovery, and record who saw it in Campaign continuity.

All six texts are **NEW campaign development**. The numbered labels help the DM match them to Sites 3–8; give players only the individual text below the divider. These handouts supplement investigation and can be copied, translated, or read aloud without a check. They never require possession of Echo's Whisper.

## Handout 1 — Catalogue Leaf Nine

*Recovered from the Silent Library's reading arm.*

---

The central entry is counted among the Nine.

Eight leaves surround it. Do not add the center a second time.

Under **RECORDED**, enter the identity.

Under **SOUNDING**, enter the condition observed and the date.

A sleeping place does not cease to exist. A named place has not necessarily answered.

The last leaf remains blank. A blank is not an invitation to invent an answer.

---

## Handout 2 — A Keeper's Tide Record

*Recovered in a ceramic document sleeve at the Drowned Observatory.*

---

Third watch. Water below the lower stair. West frame steady.

Fourth watch. Water at the scored line. Two readings from one lamp. The frame has shifted again.

Fifth watch. Shutter closed. The paired response continues.

We must record water and light separately. Otherwise the next keeper will blame the sky for a fault beneath their feet.

Lower margin, in another hand:

**SITE FOUR**

**UNSUITABLE.**

**DO NOT RETURN.**

---

## Handout 3 — Seven Nights in the Valley

*A page copied from a child's notebook at Pale Ford.*

---

First night: rain. No bell in my sleep.

Second night: washed the porch. No bell.

Third night: dry wind from the white field. Bell twice. Mother heard it too.

Fourth night: slept in the grain hall. Dreamed about our goat instead.

Fifth night: took Father's coat inside. Bell again. His coat was white at the cuffs.

Sixth night: Teren washed everything. No bell.

Seventh night: somebody said the bell knows our names.

It has never said mine.

## Handout 4 — Exterior Maintenance Plaque

*Copied from an ice-covered plaque beside the Frozen Door.*

---

The closure is deliberate.

Inspection is to be made from this side.

Check the three markers before disturbing the face. Compare the still thread against the moving thread. Replace the cracked seat before the thaw.

Do not mistake the absence of a sound for the absence of a load.

If the work cannot be completed, leave a dated warning at the outer shelter.

Those who follow deserve more than our confidence.

---

## Handout 5 — Looking Glass Packing Slip

*Tied around the curved fragment in the Walking Tower.*

---

LOOKING GLASS — RETURNED FITTING

One curved piece. One brass collar. Packing cloth to remain between stone and metal.

Copy the fitting number before removing the wrapping. Record every change of custody.

This parcel does not include the assembly instructions.

Do not infer the use of the whole from the behavior of this piece.

Later pencil note:

The collar will hold the floor until we have a proper weight. Mark its mass before anyone borrows it again.

---

## Handout 6 — The Three-Horizon Index

*Etched on a brass strip at the Broken Star's arrival shelf.*

---

Name the ledge before naming a direction.

One slit. One horizon. One observation.

Close the first before opening the second. If all three agree too neatly, check the shutters.

The shadows turn before the pieces align. Return to cover when the third shadow crosses the first.

Leave a tether for the journey back.

If you find our instrument, preserve the measurements you can repeat. Cross out the conclusions you cannot.
