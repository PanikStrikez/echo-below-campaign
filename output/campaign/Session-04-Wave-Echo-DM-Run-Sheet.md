### Arrival · follow a route the party actually earned

**Map / scene:** lmop-cave-entrance

**TV:** Preview cave art before showing it. Reveal the journey-map destination only after the characters know the route.

**Say**

> The foothills close around the trail. Ahead, a dark opening cuts beneath the rock. Cold air brushes past you, and from somewhere below comes a low crash, like surf heard through the earth.

**Ask**

- Who leads, who carries the map, and what lights the way? Are you entering to rescue someone, confront the Spider, explore the mine, or all three?

**Run**

- The mine lies about 15 miles east of Phandalin in the Sword Mountains. Gundren’s map or another established source leads to area 1; possession of a rumor alone does not reveal a precise entrance.
- General features: cold, damp, drafty; no light unless the room says otherwise. Tunnels are normally 10 feet high, rooms 20 feet, caverns 30 feet. Doors are heavy stone slabs; most passages have rough stone walls.
- Use the private p. 43 plan for connections and distance: one printed square is 10 feet. The unmarked TV maps are interpretations. State the distance and exits when the art differs.
- About every two minutes, a distant surge shakes the silence, louder toward the northeast. It comes from the subterranean water in area 16; it does not prove an Echo phenomenon.
- Choose an actual passage after each room. Number order is a reference, not a required route.

**DM source and boundaries**

### Area 8 · Fungi cavern and poison gas

**Map / scene:** lmop-map-wave-south · **Encounter:** wave-8

**TV:** E08 before disturbance; E08B only after gas is released. Stage affected characters before resolving saves.

**Say**

> Pale caps, thick stalks, and shelves of fungus crowd the cavern. Some growths glow green, casting enough light to reveal the far wall. The floor between them is almost lost beneath the carpet.

**Ask**

- How do you cross without disturbing the growth? Do you test the air, scout a route, or seek another passage?

**Run**

- Most fungi are harmless, and the glow lights the whole cavern. Attempting to cross disturbs the carpet and releases poison gas.
- Each creature in the cavern makes DC 11 Constitution: failure deals 3d6 poison and causes Poisoned for 10 minutes; success avoids these effects. Do not apply half damage on success.
- Gas disperses after 1 minute. Until then, each living creature ending its turn in the cavern repeats the save. Review poison immunities and resistances separately for each target.
- The book does not give a fixed DC for every invented crossing method. Decide whether the plan avoids disturbance before rolling, state any ruling, and preserve player alternatives.
- Record gas start/end time and each character’s condition. Pebble’s mineral interest is not evidence that this fungus is edible or that an unconfirmed ancestry trait grants poison immunity.

**DM source and boundaries**

- Lost Mine of Phandelver pp. 45–46. Gas is a hazard, not a creature and not a compulsory fight.

### Area 12 · Smelter cavern

**Map / scene:** lmop-map-wave-smelter · **Encounter:** wave-12

**TV:** Show E12 when the skull is visible; use the smelter map for channel depth and targets.

**Say**

- Area 17 provides another route around these guardians. Do not force the party to win this encounter to reach the Forge.

### Area 14 · Mormesk and the wizards’ quarters

**Map / scene:** lmop-map-wave-central · **Encounter:** wave-14

**TV:** Use E14 first; E14B when Mormesk manifests. Keep the chest and hidden map private until discovered.

**Say**

> Charred furniture stands beneath a sagging ceiling. A scorched chest waits beside a ruined bed. As you step in, darkness gathers out of the floor and turns toward you.

**Ask**

- Do you answer the spirit, offer something, withdraw, or threaten it? Has anyone touched its possessions?

**Run**

- The damaged doors need DC 15 Strength to wrench or smash open. Mormesk the wraith rises when a living creature enters. He attacks if no one attempts to reason with him.
- If neither he nor his property has been harmed, he listens. A useful magic item, scroll, spellbook, or arcane knowledge plus DC 10 Charisma (Persuasion) can persuade him of an offering’s value.
- After accepting a gift, he permits examination of his books and lets the party keep the hidden map. He offers the coins and gems for killing the spectator in area 15, but never willingly gives up his wooden pipe.
- Unlocked chest: 1,100 cp, 160 sp, 50 ep, three diamonds worth 100 gp each, and a platinum-decorated wooden pipe worth 150 gp. Reward access depends on the actual bargain or defeat.
- DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) reveals a map sewn into one preserved book. Its destination is a DM-created future dungeon; record your chosen location before publishing it.
- Mormesk is dangerous and self-interested, not a trustworthy guide to the whole mine. Track Life Drain’s maximum-HP reduction if combat occurs.

**Possible next steps**

- A gift can buy access without combat.
- Rejecting his request to kill the guardian remains a valid choice.
- A map can seed the next campaign chapter after the party chooses to follow it.

**DM source and boundaries**

- Lost Mine of Phandelver p. 47; wraith appendix B. Any Waterdeep or Echo destination assigned to the sewn map is a labeled campaign addition.

### Area 15 · Forge of Spells and its guardian

**Map / scene:** lmop-map-wave-forge · **Encounter:** wave-15

**TV:** E15 shows the guardian, so display it only when the room can be seen. Use the close-up map if positions matter.

**Say**

> Scorched worktables ring a workshop that should have fallen silent centuries ago. Yet green flame still moves in a brazier at its heart. Above it, a many-eyed creature turns, and a greeting sounds inside your mind.

**Ask**

- Do you speak to the guardian, study the fire, show credentials, or reach for something?

**Run**

- One spectator guards the workshop and still believes the mine operates. It does not attack merely because visitors speak; it attacks if they try to remove anything.
- DC 15 Charisma (Deception) can convince it that one or more characters are wizards or miners authorized by the owners to end its employment. On success it returns to its home plane. Blinding it also makes it leave, believing it can no longer perform its task.
- The north room’s door needs DC 15 Strength to force; the western double doors are damaged but slightly open. The northern workroom holds no additional keyed treasure.
- DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) identifies the brazier as the magical source. The depleted Forge cannot make permanent enchantments. A nonmagical weapon or armor bathed in its flame for at least 1 minute becomes +1 for 1d12 hours. The brazier cannot be removed.
- The southeast table holds Lightbringer (+1 mace; commanded torch-like light; +1d6 radiant against undead while glowing) and Dragonguard (+1 breastplate; advantage on saves against dragon breath weapons). Claim only after resolving the guardian and record the actual bearer.
- Use the Forge-use card for temporary items and expiry. Do not stack another +1 onto an already magical item or treat the fire as a permanent upgrade.

**Possible next steps**

- Discharge the guardian through deception, depart peacefully, or fight if the party chooses.
- Mormesk’s demand is optional; the spectator can leave without being slain.

**DM source and boundaries**

- Lost Mine of Phandelver p. 48. Negotiation, blinding, temporary enchantment, and the two named items are printed content. Any later Forge restoration project requires a separate campaign ruling.

### Area 16 · Booming cavern

**Map / scene:** lmop-map-wave-north

**TV:** Show E16 and only the ledge the party has reached. Keep water volume below table conversation.

**Say**

> The passage ends above churning water. A surge races through the dark and strikes beneath the ledge with a crash you feel in your chest. Then the water sinks back, leaving a long, hollow roar.

**Ask**

- Do you watch a full cycle, test the ledge, enter the water, or turn back?
