# Waterdeep campaign maps

Files: [City overview](Waterdeep-City-Map.png) · [Home neighborhood](Waterdeep-Neighborhood-Map.png) · [Generation prompts](Map-Prompts.md).

Two player-facing illustrations: a city overview and a close view of the party’s home neighborhood. These interpret Waterdeep’s broad geography and add our campaign businesses. They are not official street maps, measured travel maps, or gridded battle maps. Street arrangements around the custom businesses are new campaign design.

## Shared map key

| Pin | Destination | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Yawning Portal | Party home base; Durnan; Gilded Compass; descent into Undermountain |
| 2 | Second Margin / Starcup Roof | Selise’s bookshop and rooftop observatory, in the same building |
| 3 | Ninepin Works | Orren’s workshop in the Trades Ward |
| 4 | Blue Lantern Quay | Vess’s berth in the Dock Ward |
| 5 | Red Sash | Mending shop in the Dock Ward |
| 6 | Reed & Ledger | Copying house in the Castle Ward |
| 7 | Lantern House | Hospice in the Southern Ward |

The neighborhood map enlarges pins 1, 2, and 6. It places Second Margin northeast of the Portal and Reed & Ledger east of the bookshop. Other destinations require travel into their respective wards; they are not all squeezed onto the same street.

## Using the maps

The overview can be shared as the party’s city guide. The close-up supports arrival scenes, shopping, meetings, and neighborhood exploration. Both omit the Silent Library, secret affiliations, and concealed routes. A marked public business does not reveal an NPC’s private residence or allegiance. Treat unlabeled buildings as ordinary city life until needed.

The large city map prioritizes recognizable geography and our recurring destinations over exact published street placement. Use a published Waterdeep map when exact canonical addresses or distances matter. The neighborhood layout is our campaign interpretation; its street connections are not a claim about the official city plan.

Broad setting reference: [D&D Beyond’s introduction to Waterdeep](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/243-welcome-to-waterdeep-an-introduction-to-the-city). Campaign businesses and their ward assignments come from the accompanying Waterdeep Hub chapter. Illustrations generated with the built-in image-generation tool.
