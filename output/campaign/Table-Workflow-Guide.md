# Table workflow upgrades

Refresh the DM page and each connected phone to load the new controls. No phone-service restart is needed for this update.

## Guided session workflow · 30 September 2026

Refresh the DM app and use **Session guide** beside **Focus mode**. The guide lives in the right sidebar, leaving the map visible. Return to it after using any linked tool. Existing controls remain available under their original menus.

### Before play

1. **Run table check** checks the TV, phones, sound sample, current session maps/audio, and a recovery copy. Expand a warning for its fix button; confirm the sample only after hearing it. Reports belong to this campaign/session and become stale after 15 minutes. If intentionally playing without a device, describe your fallback under **Playing with an exception**; this preserves the warning.
2. Review the opening recap, drawn from the latest session with approved shared outcomes. Edit your read-aloud wording if needed. Private discoveries do not enter this automatic draft.
3. Review the active objectives and assigned quest lead; **Review quest board** returns to the existing preparation workspace.
4. **Begin this evening** creates a recovery copy and records the party and campaign state from that point onward. Earlier changes are not reconstructed. The live record resumes after reloading.

### During play

**Play** shows the current scene, selected notes, objective, next useful action and relevant pending decisions. **Explore**, **Converse** and **Combat** open the existing scene or combat guide. Use **Preview scene / encounter** to stage the map, enemies, opening and optional audio; its explicit TV approval still controls the reveal.

Use **Record an outcome** for discoveries, rescue/escape results, promises and important item transfers. The existing outcome review controls knowledge, linked quests and publication. Main, side and character quest routing is unchanged. Additional tools stay folded under **More tools**.

### After play

**Close** gathers HP/resources, hit dice, Inspiration, money/equipment, party loot, discoveries, quest progress, promises, NPC changes, encounter aftermath notes, scratch notes and unresolved items. Categories stay collapsed until opened. Add review notes, use the original editors to correct live records, or turn notes into a private outcome draft for the existing approval workflow. Editing review notes alone does not alter character statistics or approve campaign history.

Record the stopping point and where to resume. Review pending requests individually or explicitly carry the listed items into the next session. Changed requests need renewed acknowledgement. **I reviewed these changes** enables **Save review & end evening** when all required items have been considered. Further changes invalidate that review. Closing preserves unfinished combat, resources and requests and keeps the review private.

Saved reviews can be reopened for corrections, and **Prepare a new evening** starts a fresh comparison in the same saved session while keeping the previous reviews. Combat Undo/Redo preserves the evening baseline and notes. Full campaign backups include these records; practice copies start with their own empty evening history.

**Limits:** The guide records changes from when you begin; it cannot infer unrecorded table events. Recap notes open prefilled in the existing recap editor and require separate review/publication. Phone freshness and audible speaker output still need a check at the real table. Material checks cover the existing session file checker, not completeness of every possible future branch or automatic encounter balancing.

**Verification:** An isolated browser practice campaign completed the opening check, exploration, conversation with an approved promise, private encounter staging and deliberate TV reveal, party/NPC initiative, reviewed damage, next turn, carried requests and active combat, an edited closing review, and reload. Tests also checked source privacy, stale-review prevention, separate sessions, repeated evenings, backup normalization, and actual combat Undo/Redo. The real campaign, phone service and source books were not changed by the rehearsal.

## Personal quick bar

The row beneath the main controls starts with music, reveal tools, potion/item use, damage/healing and private clues. **Edit quick bar** lets you pin, unpin and reorder actions with ↑ and ↓. Preferences stay in this browser. Shortcuts open a tool or review; they do not automatically reveal fog, spend items or send clues.

**Potion / item use** chooses the user, recipient and held item, then opens the existing consumption/healing review. Assign unclaimed party potions to a carrier and correct unknown quantities first.

## Scene workspaces

Changing locations remembers the outgoing scene's selected runbook note, expanded notes, notes scroll position, visual search/type filter and applicable right-panel tab. Returning restores them. The map's existing per-scene pan and zoom remain in the campaign save. DM layout preferences are local to this browser and separated by campaign/session/scene. Opening a preview alone does not change the TV or the current location.

## Guided combat

The active-turn attack panel now starts with **Use this action**, requires an explicit target, then proceeds through roll, reaction confirmation, damage and final review. The damage review shows HP/temporary HP changes, defenses and attack usage before applying. A resolved event shows **Done**. Existing reaction pauses and stale-preview checks remain in force.

Phone actions show the same sequence and retain their combined resource, concentration, usage and effect preview. Spell-specific exceptions, unusual feature costs and ammunition still require the relevant sheet/action review; this does not invent rules for incomplete cards.

## Notifications

The inbox separates **Needs your decision now** from **Requests & ordinary updates**. Similar updates are grouped by kind and scene, while every individual request keeps its own Review button. Items from another scene are labeled. An unchanged inbox is not rebuilt on every refresh.

## Players went elsewhere

This button keeps your prepared runbook beat while gathering known destinations, connected routes, location-matching NPCs, and clues not recorded as seen. Clue candidates are explicitly campaign-wide, not assertions that an item is physically at the new location. Preview a destination privately, plan travel, save an improv note, or convert it into a permanent campaign entry. **Return to prepared scene notes** restores your notes without moving the TV to a different location.

## Phone walkthrough

A character's first visit offers five short steps: confirm character, find actions, roll dice, request movement and read private clues. **Show me where** navigates to the relevant panel. It never rolls or submits requests. Players can skip or restart using **Phone walkthrough**. Completion is remembered separately for each character on that phone.

## Session changes

**Session changes** compares current records with a saved baseline: HP, temporary HP, conditions, character resources, money/equipment, party loot, private clues, evidence, NPC interactions/promises and continuity events. It links to the original editors for corrections and the inbox for unresolved requests. The closing draft includes this comparison, and the existing save action creates a recovery snapshot.

Tracking begins when this upgrade first opens a session. It does not reconstruct prior play. Save your closing review before choosing **Start a new comparison from now** for the next sitting.

## Practice campaign

Choose **Settings → Create practice campaign**. It creates separate campaign/session save records from the current campaign and clears the copied comparison baseline. The DM screen, player TV and phone session title identify practice play. The banner has a **Return to real campaign** button.

Entering or leaving practice closes phone access, drains pending phone processing, and requires starting phone access and scanning a new QR code for that campaign. This prevents queued practice requests from crossing into the real campaign. If phone processing cannot finish, the switch stops. Practice saves remain available through the normal campaign selector; they can be archived using the existing campaign tools.
