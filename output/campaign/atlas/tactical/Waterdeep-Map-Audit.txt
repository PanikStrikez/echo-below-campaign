PRIVATE PLAY GUIDANCE AND NEIGHBORHOOD SERVICES
The follow-up audit found that the playable art lacked operating notes and the
neighborhood lacked useful service waypoints. These are now private metadata;
all 14 SVG files have identical hashes before and after this addition.

Each map has playNotes with summary, cover, elevation, movement, furniture and
sceneUses. Notes distinguish substantial furniture from soft/open objects and
require the DM to check actual lines and positions before applying the existing
cover rules. No bonus is granted automatically. Optional stair/rim/tide heights
are explicitly authored staging; the source-grounded Portal dimensions remain
40-foot diameter and 140-foot shaft. Empty lodging includes the possibility of
agreed secured storage, with no containers, contents or furniture acquired.

Neighborhood preparation contains four service cards using existing approved
NPC IDs: Nessa's ordinary binding work; Jori/Pellan's meals and packed food;
Orenna's packing/local-delivery advice; and Aven's copying or agreed observation
at the shared landing. The cards link to existing permitted boards. Availability,
prices and agreements are null. They establish no introduction, presence, free
service, tenancy, custody, knowledge of a private object or current obligation.

Two arrival waypoints and a proposed walking line connect the court passage to
the street and the off-map tavern direction. No mileage, exact bearing, named
street or travel time is invented. Private markers and that route are supplied
for the atlas builder/UI; neither belongs in the public map payload.

Waterdeep-Preparation-Validation.json records 223 focused passing checks for the
new notes, existing NPC identities, unconfirmed terms, links and exclusion from
player projections. The prior 717 geometry/privacy checks were not repeated for
this metadata-only addition. App integration separately checks the private cards
and markers after the atlas data refresh.
