# Opening encounter balance: five player characters

**Historical reference:** preserves the level and information at the time. Current five-person level-3 sheets and the latest stopping point are in Current-Party.md and Session-02-03-Actual-Recap.md. Simulated playtests do not establish actual events.

*DM-facing notes for the Lost Mine of Phandelver opening. Pebble’s provisional numbers are in his handout; use Aldric’s rolled level-1 build for initial pacing and accept any corrected sheet from Bowman.*

Trista, Donavan, and Ringgo are level 1. Pebble joins in Session 1 as a level-1 fighter (AC 18, HP 12, Second Wind, Interception) according to his handout. Aldric joins as a level-1 wizard with HP 8, AC 11 before Mage Armor, two 1st-level slots, and no healing spells on the starting sheet. Dracuseth is in Waterdeep and is **not** a combatant or source of temporary HP or healing during the opening.

## First encounter

Run the published four-goblin road ambush as written. Ask all five players for marching order and watch positions. Resolve surprise from each character’s actual passive Perception. Give the goblins their printed attacks, cover, and retreat behavior. This first fight tells you more than an estimate based on two unknown sheets.

Five PCs usually have more actions than the original three-PC party, so the ambush may end quickly. Let a clean win stand. The rescue lead, trail, traps, wolf kennel, flood, Yeemik’s bargain, and Klarg’s cave provide later decisions and danger. Do not increase a goblin’s or Klarg’s damage to compensate for party size; level-1 characters can still fall to a few hits.

## After you have all five sheets

Record each character’s level, maximum and current HP, AC, initiative bonus, passive Perception, healing, and limited-use resources. Check whether the two newcomers have strong area attacks or healing. Reassess later encounter pacing with positioning, warnings, morale, negotiation, and rests before adding enemies. If you change a roster, record the change on that encounter card and keep the reason visible to yourself.

## Safety and choice during play

- Show goblin cover and routes once the characters can perceive them; hidden enemies stay hidden until discovered.
- Telegraph the snare, pit, cave alarms, flood, and Klarg’s heavy attack through the adventure’s clues and fiction.
- A character at 0 HP needs death saves or a real source of aid. Dracuseth cannot arrive from Waterdeep to heal someone in Session 1.
- A secured short rest can be possible if the players create a defensible place. Time, noise, and living enemies still matter.
- Sildar’s rescue can be achieved by stealth, negotiation, retreat and return, or combat. Clearing every room is not required for a satisfying Session 1.

Use the [Session 1 run sheet](Session-01-DM-Run-Sheet.md) and the adventure’s printed room keys for exact encounter details.
