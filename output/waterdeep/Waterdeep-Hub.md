# The Echo Below — Waterdeep Hub

Campaign Bible expansion • DM edition • Includes secrets

Visual references: [Recurring cast](Waterdeep-Cast.png) · [Locations and Echo’s Whisper](Waterdeep-Locations-and-Medallion.png) · [Generation prompts and art notes](Visual-Prompts.md). The location plate reveals the hidden archive; show it selectively. Incidental lettering in the artwork does not establish additional lore.

Waterdeep is where the characters build lives worth coming back to. Undermountain provides the central mystery; the city supplies friendship, rivalries, practical help, and choices about what that mystery should become.

## Continuity and source key

**BOOK** means published setting/adventure material. **ESTABLISHED** means custom material recovered from “DND Campaign Help.” **NEW** means campaign additions developed in this chapter. All unmarked NPC secrets, dialogue, businesses, relationships, clues, and progression below are NEW. They are not claims about published lore. Character levels below are suggested timing, not dungeon-floor numbers.

**BOOK:** Durnan operates the Yawning Portal, whose well provides entry into Undermountain. Halaster and the dungeon belong to *Waterdeep: Dungeon of the Mad Mage*. Its dungeon history supplies the knot in the Weave; the Echo network is our interpretation layered onto that foundation. Keep the adventure’s room descriptions, residents, travel restrictions, and rules unless deliberately modifying them. The Harpers are a published faction; Ilvara and this local operation are custom.

**ESTABLISHED:** Lost Mine of Phandelver → Waterdeep → Dungeon of the Mad Mage; milestone advancement; Waterdeep is home. The Echo is resonance produced by the knot. There are **nine total sites: Undermountain plus eight satellites**. Sites are not a portal network. Halaster identified them without visiting all of them. Nezznar independently follows research and does not serve Halaster. Gundren is Trista’s benefactor; he acquired the black stone medallion without understanding it. Mara likes Trista but steals because she can, rather than to help others, and receives a contract to steal the medallion.

**Continuity correction already adopted in the prior conversation:** retire the early one-plus-nine and one-plus-twenty-three versions of the medallion. Use one center plus eight surrounding circles everywhere. If a twenty-three-part dungeon diagram has already appeared at the table, treat it as a separate dungeon survey, not a different form of the Nine symbol.

**Party now established:** Trista, Pebble, Donavan Kemp, Ringgo Paul, and Aldric Vane are the current five-person party. Preserve the player-held details still marked open in the continuity reference rather than treating any character as unfinished. The Hollow Mountain’s location and personal connection remain open. Echo’s Whisper’s mechanical powers remain subject to balancing. Looking Glass was designed to amplify the knot; why it was abandoned and the exact consequences of completing it remain mysteries. Do not establish a mandatory ending or make every NPC secretly serve Halaster.

## Eight recurring faces

### 1. Durnan — the person who expects you back

**Status:** BOOK NPC; the following relationships, scenes, and progression are NEW. **Role:** innkeeper, home-base anchor, experienced observer. **Base/home:** the Yawning Portal; use its published layout for private quarters.

**Appearance direction:** broad, weathered human man, dark hair threaded with gray, plain working clothes, economical movement. His hands stop polishing a tankard before his expression changes. This is campaign art direction, not an exact reproduction of official art.

**Personality and voice:** dry, practical, observant; kindness expressed through food and preparedness. “Tell someone when you expect to be back.” He dislikes bragging about casualties and respects people who retrieve someone else’s pack.

**Relationships:** trusts Vess’s judgment about expeditions; allows Kestrel’s crew to boast until they torment novices; knows Mara is trouble but does not confuse suspicion with proof. He comes to trust the PCs through their conduct, not their wealth.

**DM secret:** he has heard the party’s peculiar two-beat description from previous survivors. He kept a page from an abandoned expedition log because its owner never returned. He does not know what the Nine mean.

**Clue:** the log records an anomalous pulse below the well, corroborating Site 1. A later margin entry mentions a traveler comparing the pulse to a flooded coastal ruin: a backup lead to Site 4.

**Progression:** levels 5–8, he learns their names and stores a sealed emergency letter; 9–14, he reserves their usual table and notices when their behavior changes; 15–20, he helps organize shelter and supplies if the city is threatened. He does not descend to solve their campaign.

**First scene:** he asks for a return date, then puts an extra bowl on the table for the companion still washing dungeon blood off their hands. **Pressure:** a frightened survivor wants the well closed. Durnan asks the party for evidence and a practical response.

### 2. Selise Ardent — the scholar decoding the Nine

**Status:** NEW. **Role:** translator, cartographic historian, careful theorist. **Base/home:** The Second Margin bookshop in the Castle Ward; a cramped room above it and access to the Starcup Roof.

**Appearance:** older brown-skinned human woman; silver braid, brass spectacles, indigo shawl, ink on two fingers. Her desk has three trays marked observed, inferred, and disputed.

**Personality and voice:** delighted by precision, embarrassed by praise, fiercely protective of students. “That is an excellent theory. What would prove it wrong?” She can explain inscriptions, but cannot conjure answers without evidence.

**Relationships:** Orren tests her theories; Ilvara supplies records but wants names redacted; Anja helped her through an illness. Selise thinks Mara’s provenance stories are terrible and buys useful documents anyway.

**DM secret:** a mentor’s disappearance led her to buy this shop. An old floor plan has a room that the current building lacks. She has not found the Silent Library and does not know its society’s fate.

**Clues:** a blank book reacts near Echo’s Whisper, showing the established first/second/third inscription. Building deeds locate the missing staircase. Her dated tower sketches lead to Site 7; inconsistent star observations point toward Site 8.

**Progression:** 5–8, establishes that the mark describes a relationship rather than a noble crest; the Silent Library confirms nine sites. 9–14, distinguishes a site waking from several sites synchronizing. 15–20, prepares a comparative dossier for the party’s final decision, explicitly marking uncertainty.

**First scene:** she asks permission to copy the medallion rather than reaching for it. **Pressure:** a wealthy sponsor offers to fund her work in exchange for exclusive access. The party can offer alternatives; accepting sponsorship does not automatically make her a traitor.

### 3. Mara Vey — the friend who might take it

**Status:** ESTABLISHED character and theft contract; appearance, employer, and local details NEW. **Role:** Trista’s criminal contact, temptation, information broker. **Base/home:** a rented attic above the custom Red Sash mending shop in the Dock Ward; meetings on the Blue Lantern Quay or in a public booth at the Portal.

**Appearance:** lean human woman in her thirties, olive skin, short dark waves, eyebrow scar, charcoal coat, burgundy scarf. Her jewelry is inexpensive; her boots are excellent.

**Personality and voice:** playful, incisive, unsentimental about property. “You give it away. I call that skipping the best part.” She genuinely enjoys Trista and resents being treated as a moral lesson.

**Relationships:** former partner of Trista; occasionally sells to Orren; trades favors with Vess. Ilvara keeps her at arm’s length. A broker associated with Anja’s secret faction offers the medallion contract; Mara initially knows only the broker.

**DM secret:** the price is extravagantly high for something the client calls dangerous junk. Mara investigates before committing to delivery. She is not secretly responsible for Gundren’s earlier troubles.

**Clues:** the contract’s packaging instructions specify ceramic insulation and suppression of vibration. A warehouse receipt connects the broker to hospice deliveries. Her stolen salvage list supplies a second route to Site 4 and an auction entry supplies Site 6’s expedition journal.

**Progression:** 5–8, reunites with Trista and probes what the medallion does; 9–14, the contract forces a choice between profit, loyalty, and fear; 15–20, she may become a trusted smuggler, an estranged rival, or a negotiable opponent. Decide from play, not a compulsory betrayal.

**First scene:** she returns a small object she lifted from Trista years ago, then asks about Gundren. **Run the theft fairly:** show the offer, surveillance, or questions first; resolve any attempt through play. Never declare an unavoidable offscreen loss of the signature item.

### 4. Orren Brassmantle — the expert who asks what an item costs its owner

**Status:** NEW. **Role:** magic-item examiner, repairer, maker of research instruments. **Base/home:** Ninepin Works in the Trades Ward; family living rooms above the workshop.

**Appearance:** stocky dwarf man, copper-brown skin, auburn beard threaded with ceramic beads, leather apron, jeweler’s loupe. Brass tools sit in felt-lined drawers; delicate objects rest on ceramic rather than metal.

**Personality and voice:** patient, tactile, quietly funny. “I can tell you what it does. Why someone made it is a different invoice.” He refuses destructive tests without consent.

**Relationships:** Selise’s friendly intellectual sparring partner; repairs Vess’s instruments; buys legitimate salvage from Kestrel. He admires Anja’s medical work and does not know who funds some of her orders.

**DM secret:** he has already built ceramic damping housings for a buyer who claimed they were medical equipment. Their measurements match the medallion’s diameter. He kept the order because it was unusually specific.

**Clues:** forge residue from Site 2 and the medallion produce related responses under controlled comparison. His invoice corroborates Mara’s contract. A recovered star-glass sample points to Site 8 only after Selise compares it to observations.

**Progression:** 5–8, records the dormant/awakened appearance without granting new powers; 9–14, builds a limited research apparatus that compares samples locally; 15–20, helps prototype a sealing or retuning component chosen by the PCs. No device becomes a universal detector or instant shutdown button.

**First scene:** he asks Trista to keep hold of the cord while he examines the stone. **Pressure:** a profitable commission would require sacrificing an irreplaceable artifact. He wants a second opinion, not someone to make his decision for him.

### 5. Ilvara Reed — a faction ally with boundaries

**Status:** NEW representative of the published Harpers; this mission is custom. **Role:** records access, discreet investigation, civic consequences. **Base:** a rented office at the custom Reed & Ledger copying house in the Castle Ward. **Home:** an ordinary rented North Ward apartment; its address is not a reward for faction service.

**Appearance:** middle-aged dark-skinned half-elf woman, cropped curls, blue-gray traveling coat, silver harp pin worn inside her lapel.

**Personality and voice:** composed, direct, suspicious of grand solutions. “Who benefits if we believe this?” She respects refusals and requests reciprocal discretion.

**Relationships:** supplies Selise with copies; regards Mara as a source rather than a recruit; has paid Kestrel for a rescue. She trusts Anja’s patient care but questions unexplained supply purchases.

**DM secret:** reports of magical anomalies have been diverted through a false clerical routing order. She suspects a leak but has no grounds to accuse a named person. This is a local breach, not proof that the Harpers are compromised as a whole.

**Clues:** witness statements locate Site 5’s affected valley; travel records corroborate Site 6; reports of the same impossible tower at different dates help locate Site 7. She supplies mundane records for Site 9 only once play establishes its personal connection.

**Progression:** 5–8, offers one bounded information exchange; 9–14, entrusts the party with sources and civilian consequences; 15–20, can coordinate allies and evacuation if relationships support it. Faction membership is optional and confers no immunity from city law.

**First scene:** asks them to recover a missing surveyor’s notebook, not swear an oath. **Pressure:** publishing the network map might expose people living near its sites. Give the PCs a meaningful choice about redaction.

### 6. Kestrel Dane — captain of the Gilded Compass

**Status:** NEW. **Role:** recurring rival expedition leader; the principal face of a small supporting party. **Base/home:** rented rooms at the Yawning Portal and a shared storage shed near the docks.

**Appearance:** young adult human woman, freckled tan skin, tied-back red hair, dented blue breastplate, a gold cloth strip at her shoulder. Her smile is confident; her repaired equipment betrays a thin budget.

**Personality and voice:** competitive, generous in public, afraid of being ordinary. “Second back buys breakfast.” She wants recognition and reliable pay, not the PCs’ deaths.

**The Gilded Compass:** Kestrel handles negotiation and the front line. Pell, a soft-spoken halfling scout in a moss cloak, sketches routes and argues for retreat. Nym, a copper-scaled dragonborn wizard in patched plum robes, catalogs objects and hates breaking them. These are supporting faces, keeping the core recurring cast at eight. All wear a small brass compass badge with one bent point; it is unrelated to the Nine.

**Relationships:** Durnan monitors their bravado; Orren buys salvage; Vess once rescued them from a disastrous charter. Kestrel envies the PCs’ attention from Selise while liking the party more than she intended.

**DM secret:** she sold the original northern expedition journal to pay for an injured companion’s treatment, retaining a partial copy. She bluffs about the copy being complete.

**Clues:** the copy launches Site 6; dates in Pell’s travel sketches independently corroborate Site 7. If the players miss the Silent Library lead, Nym asks why a purchased book contains numbered blank pages.

**Progression:** 5–8, friendly contests and rumors; 9–14, a failed expedition makes cooperation necessary; 15–20, they can secure an evacuation route or handle a secondary objective while the PCs confront the central threat. They never clear a major site offscreen or steal a climax.

**First scene:** Kestrel offers a wager on who returns with the strangest harmless souvenir. **Pressure:** a sponsor demands an artifact be delivered unopened. Kestrel wants the party to examine it without publicly embarrassing her.

### 7. Sister Anja Vale — compassion with a dangerous conclusion

**Status:** NEW. **Role:** healer, community anchor, hidden link to the faction that wants the network destroyed. **Base/home:** Lantern House, a custom charitable hospice in the Southern Ward; a small cell beside its herb courtyard. “Sister” is a community title; her faith can be chosen later without redefining a published temple.

**Appearance:** older human woman, weathered pale face, gray bun, cream clothing and moss-green stole. She looks tired and approachable. Do not visually telegraph a villain.

**Personality and voice:** attentive, unhurried, uncomfortably persuasive. “Tell me who would be standing nearest when it fails.” Her care for patients is real.

**Relationships:** nursed Selise; buys Orren’s damping housings through a broker; has treated Kestrel’s companions. She recognizes Trista’s charitable motives and offers sincere ways to help.

**DM secret:** Anja belongs to the **Still Hand**, the new name for the established anti-network faction. She supplies testimony and refuge; its acquisition broker issues Mara’s contract. She knows of the purchase but initially not the intended thief. Years ago she treated survivors of a resonance accident whose cause remains incompletely understood. Halaster did not secretly engineer her life.

**Clues:** patient accounts of shared dreams lead to Site 5. Her notes distinguish ancient sealing methods at Site 6 from the modern Still Hand; the faction did not necessarily build that seal. Her supplies and a duplicate phrase in the contract expose the organizational link.

**Progression:** 5–8, trusted healer and advocate; 9–14, evidence reveals the affiliation and she argues for severance; 15–20, she can support a tested safer alternative or oppose the party’s plan through disclosed, consequential action. Her reaction depends on their evidence and conduct.

**First scene:** asks for clean bandages and assistance delivering food before requesting any artifact. **Pressure:** her faction wants to destroy a component she believes might also protect patients. She can dissent. She will not poison patients or make lifesaving care conditional on allegiance.

### 8. Captain Rusk Vess — the way back to the wider world

**Status:** NEW. **Role:** transport contact, witness, practical expedition planner. **Base:** Blue Lantern Quay in the Dock Ward. **Home:** her cutter, the *Patient Gull*.

**Appearance:** older broad half-orc woman, gray-green skin, salt-white braid, one brass-capped tusk, navy wool coat. Her charts smell of cloves and tar.

**Personality and voice:** blunt, superstitious about small things, rational about large dangers. “A map is what someone survived long enough to write down.” She negotiates terms openly.

**Relationships:** long-standing acquaintance of Durnan; occasional transport for Mara; protective of Kestrel’s inexperienced crew; brings Orren broken compasses.

**DM secret:** she lost a friend near the Drowned Observatory and falsified the public position to deter treasure hunters. She retains the true bearings. Earning her trust means presenting a rescue and return plan, not winning one persuasion roll.

**Clues:** the true log, a recovered brass star plate, and the tides together give a usable route to Site 4. A later fisher’s account locates one appearance of Site 7. She can arrange the first leg north for Site 6, not deliver the party directly to the glacier.

**Progression:** 5–8, sells information and modest passage; 9–14, becomes an expedition partner; 15–20, directs ordinary transport and relief work while heroes handle extraordinary threats. Planar travel remains beyond her vessel’s normal capabilities.

**First scene:** asks the party to explain why a compass recovered from a wreck points down. **Pressure:** a creditor wants her ship. Resolving that problem creates loyalty without an Echo revelation.

### Making the Yawning Portal home

Start with rented rooms and an ordinary shared table. After the party returns reliably and treats staff well, Durnan offers a standing booking and a lockable storage chest on normal negotiated terms. Later they can rent an adjoining room as a briefing room. These are campaign additions, not ownership of the inn or a magical safe zone.

Give each character a visible personal space: a shelf, a hook for equipment, a letter tucked into a doorframe. Trista’s can accumulate thank-you notes from people she helped. Donavan’s can collect labeled field notes, letters from Dianna, and a perch for his owl. Ringgo’s begins spare and defensible: a clear view of the door, a place for tools, and one ordinary possession he chooses to leave in sight. Let later additions show when he begins to believe he is staying.

Use three recurring rituals: Durnan asks their expected return date; someone notices a new scar or souvenir; the party chooses who gets the first story over dinner. Most returns should be restful. Reserve home-base danger for an earned major escalation, rather than repeated burglaries and hostage plots.

Return travel still follows the dungeon’s actual access and magic restrictions. The Echo does not give a convenient teleport home. Summarize a secured route when appropriate; revisit it when control or hazards have materially changed.

## The Still Hand and the argument against activation

**ESTABLISHED:** a third faction wants the network destroyed and argues that the party may be completing Halaster’s work. **NEW:** its name, organization, evidence, and internal disagreements.

The Still Hand wants to sever the connections between sites and the Prime Echo. It does not need to destroy the Weave itself, and it does not know how to remove the knot. Its public-facing members support research into magical accidents. Its small acquisition cell buys, steals, and suppresses dangerous components. Anja favors informed agreement and sealing; a harder wing favors irreversible destruction. Avoid assigning every suspicious person to it.

Their strongest evidence is a controlled local experiment: when two recovered components are deliberately tuned together, the measured response grows. That supports concern about synchronization. It does **not** prove that examining a ruin or carrying the medallion empowers Halaster. Their missing evidence is what the complete network would do and whether reversible isolation can prevent it.

Reveal Anja through three observable clues: Orren’s duplicate specification, a delivery receipt tied to the acquisition broker, and her possession of a matching suppression diagram. Each alone has an innocent explanation; together they justify a conversation. She admits her affiliation when presented with credible evidence. Insight is not a mind-reading shortcut.

Her confrontation line: “If we are wrong, we lose a discovery. If you are wrong, who loses their home?” Selise answers that ignorance also has costs. Orren proposes a bounded experiment. Ilvara asks who will oversee it. Mara asks what everyone is willing to pay. Let the players decide which argument survives scrutiny.

### Track choices without fixing the ending

For each site, record: condition, people affected, knowledge recovered, custody of components, and connection state. Connection states are **unexamined, observed, deliberately tuned, isolated, or severed**. This is NEW DM bookkeeping, not a player-facing subsystem or settled cosmology.

Studying a site can provide leverage and safer options. Deliberate tuning can make its power available to an amplification attempt. Isolation removes that connection while the seal holds. Severance removes it more permanently but can destroy knowledge or damage local interests. Removing a component may suppress or destabilize a site; determine and foreshadow that site’s behavior before the choice, rather than using a surprise punishment.

At the finale, translate actual decisions into available allies, shutdown options, missing power sources, and exposed civilians. Do not impose numerical encounter modifiers until the party’s level and the final design are known. Preserve destroy, complete, rewrite, and confrontation possibilities.

## Campaign progression and first visits

**Arrival, levels 5–6:** introduce Durnan, Mara, and Selise in separate scenes. Nezznar’s evidence gives a reason to consult Selise; Durnan offers the practical way down; Mara brings the personal past. Do not deliver all nine site names at once.

**First return, levels 6–8:** warm meal, one party-specific letter, and the blank-book reaction. Meet Orren through a need for examination. The Gilded Compass returns with a different account of familiar dangers. Investigating the Silent Library establishes the Nine; Vess offers the next wider-world possibility.

**Middle campaign, levels 9–14:** Ilvara connects discoveries to people outside the dungeon. Anja is already someone the party has helped before her affiliation becomes relevant. Reveal the theft contract and allow a response before it is resolved. Keep Looking Glass’s abandonment reveal tied to the established dungeon roadmap; city research contextualizes it rather than prematurely replacing it.

**Late campaign, levels 15–20:** Selise and Orren interpret evidence the PCs recovered, including the amplification finding scheduled for the deep dungeon. The hub becomes a council of distinct interests rather than a unanimous quest-giving committee. Bind Site 9 to an actual player-created relationship or consequence. Halaster still has not reached it.

On each return, prepare one welcome, one changed relationship, and at most two actionable leads. Advance consequences when enough in-world time passes or player actions cause them. Do not force every visit into a new emergency.

### Three ready-to-run scenes

**The first supper:** rain rattles the shutters while a basket descends into the well. Mara sits at the party’s table without asking. Durnan puts down bread and asks when they expect to return. If Trista carries the medallion by then, Mara notices it but asks about Gundren first. Donavan notices Mara measuring everyone’s reactions; Ringgo notices the exits she kept open. Outcome: a home, three character beats, and a reason to visit Selise; no mandatory fight.

**The book that remembers:** at Second Margin, words appear when the medallion passes a blank page. Selise asks everyone to remain still and repeats the test. A building deed provides the hidden room’s location. The party chooses whether to investigate now, secure the entrance, or return after preparing. No failed roll erases the discovery.

**A quiet accusation:** after the contract comes to light, Anja invites a conversation in the hospice courtyard. Patients continue eating nearby. She presents one documented accident and one uncertain inference; Mara can identify the broker. The scene ends with a choice about evidence-sharing, a supervised test, or refusal, not a compulsory betrayal battle.

### Equal spotlight

**Trista:** Gundren and Mara make the mystery personal; Durnan respects reliability; Anja recognizes her charity. Her question is what responsibility comes with holding something powerful. She is never the only person who can interpret evidence or decide the network’s fate.

**Donavan:** Selise challenges the precision of his claims, Orren turns theory into consent-based experiments, and Ilvara asks what responsible disclosure looks like when records can endanger sources. Dianna and the Kemp Archive keep his idea of home alive without solving field problems for him.

**Ringgo:** Mara understands the utility and danger of criminal skill, Ilvara offers a model of secrets used to protect rather than control, Vess values return plans over bravado, and Kestrel can become a rival who trusts his scouting. Anja can challenge him with the human cost of expedient methods. These are new relationships, not proof that any NPC was his former handler. Skullport may echo his old world without revealing the same organization behind every crime.

Use at least one character-specific welcome or choice on each return, rotating focus. Leave Site 9 unassigned until a relationship, promise, failure, or victory created in play makes it matter to the whole party.

## Visual bible

**Shared language:** Waterdeep is rain-dark slate, warm timber, worn brass, cream paper, and inhabited rooms. Echo effects use restrained violet-gray light and doubled shadows. Warm human spaces should remain visually distinct from cold resonance imagery. Generated plates are campaign concept art, not official portraits, canonical maps, or scale battle maps.

**Echo symbol:** one larger central circle; exactly eight smaller circles arranged around it, linked by eight fine spokes. The center is Site 1. Starting at the top and moving clockwise, the satellites represent Sites 2–9; the upper-left satellite is therefore Site 9. This positional convention is NEW and diagrams relationships, not geographical bearings. Seven satellite centers contain muted inlay; Site 9’s is hollow. A filled inlay means recorded identity, not present activation: glow and annotation indicate observed activity separately. No decorative border circles that can be mistaken for extra sites.

**Echo’s Whisper:** established black stone medallion, palm-small; NEW art direction gives it a worn cord, shallow silver-gray inlay, one chipped lower edge, and a smooth unlettered reverse. It looks handled, not jewel-encrusted. Dormant: no glow. Awakened: a brief paired glimmer. Resonant: a matching known mark responds faintly during a site encounter. Final form: leave exact transformation open; preserve the established moment when Site 9 fills. These are appearance cues, not added mechanical powers. Do not depict it as a portal key or automatic destination finder.

**Trista:** preserve her established human appearance: 5'8", pale skin, straight brown hair, brown eyes, oversized monk-style robes, calm bearing. Once she carries the medallion, it is usually tucked under the robe. Do not redesign her as an armored assassin to match Mara.

**Other props:** the BELOW token is rough dark stone with the single word and a partial, worn mark; the blank book is gray linen with no ominous title; Orren’s comparator uses brass tuning forks and removable ceramic cups; a Still Hand document bears a single short horizontal bar crossing an open arc, visibly distinct from the Nine. The Gilded Compass badge has a bent compass point, not the resonance symbol.

**Silent Library:** empty desks, pale dust undisturbed by footsteps in selected areas, book spines without titles, a circular reading table. Silence is an authored site phenomenon, not a blanket assumption that all spells fail.
