# Lost Mine of Phandelver · remaining visual guide

103 new player-facing images for the 2014 adventure, covering Phandalin, the Redbrand hideout, wilderness sites and random encounters, Thundertree, Cragmaw Castle, Wave Echo Cave, and a conditional epilogue. These supplement the 16 Session 1 illustrations.

Open **Presentation → Lost Mine art** in the campaign app to browse by chapter or search by place, room, or key. Preview an image and its reveal note on the DM screen, then press **Show this image on TV** when the party reaches it. **Return to battlefield** restores the map without changing tokens, HP, or fog. The Session 2 runbook also has a **Scene art** section beside each applicable beat, with separate Preview and Show buttons. The art for later chapters is ready in the chapter browser and in the corresponding visual content packs.

R, T, C, and E area numbers follow the printed adventure; P, W, and Z are visual catalog keys. Show a **revealed** variant only after its creature or hazard is actually seen. These are illustrations, so use the app's player-safe tactical maps when positions matter. The 2014 adventure copy under `sources/` is the room reference; the files there were not changed.

The common image prompt used the Session 1 cave image as a style reference only: player-facing 3:2 watercolor and gouache scene, delicate ink edges, parchment grain, grounded fantasy mood; no text, labels, watermark, grid, UI, or depicted player characters. The subject and reveal-specific constraints are recorded in [catalog.tsv](lmop-visuals/catalog.tsv). The prison room shows empty cells, and the Forge of Spells spectator was corrected to four eye stalks.

Quick previews: [Phandalin](lmop-visuals/contact-sheets/phandalin.jpg) · [Redbrand Hideout](lmop-visuals/contact-sheets/redbrand-hideout.jpg) · [Wilderness](lmop-visuals/contact-sheets/wilderness.jpg) · [Thundertree](lmop-visuals/contact-sheets/thundertree.jpg) · [Cragmaw Castle](lmop-visuals/contact-sheets/cragmaw-castle.jpg) · [Wave Echo Cave](lmop-visuals/contact-sheets/wave-echo-cave.jpg) · [Epilogue](lmop-visuals/contact-sheets/epilogue.jpg).
