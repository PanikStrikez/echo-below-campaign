Exploration marker choices — verified 14 October 2026

Where to find it
Explore -> Session map -> select a location or room marker.
Open scene is the primary action. Other prepared scenes, reviewed information,
contacts, session leads and alternative approaches remain expandable.
Another destination / improvise uses the existing private detour and note workflow.

What changed
Location cards use explicit quest/preparation references, open objectives,
scene questions and existing NPC records. Contacts are not assumed present.
DM-reviewed preparation is distinguished from preparation needing review.
Unresolved / Postponed / Outcome recorded are a separate DM status axis;
they never mean located, visited or cleared. Marker status uses approved factual
outcomes linked to the exact map and marker, with corrections/replacements applied.
Record or postpone this location opens a private draft. Choose the DM marker
state under Map notes and reveals, enter the actual decision and review changes.
Opening a linked scene carries that marker into scene or combat aftermath review
where the current origin still matches. Catalog navigation does not attach an
unrelated selected marker.
Private source-layout markers can receive DM record statuses but cannot be revealed.
Player maps continue using existing permitted wording/discovery projections;
DM context and status metadata are excluded from TV/phone payloads.
No new campaign outcomes, rewards, discoveries or visits were added to live data.

Limitations
Physical TV and phones were not tested. No live phone service was restarted.
Locations without explicit quest/preparation links honestly show no linked quest.
Town locations without a separate travel record do not inherit the town's visit
state. Reviewed outcome records must link the marker to appear in its status.
The unnamed inn remains unresolved; selecting the named inn's prepared scene
must not establish that it is where Gundren was left.
Books, artwork, geometry and existing saved records were preserved.
