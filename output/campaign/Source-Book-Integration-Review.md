# Source book integration review

Reviewed 23 September 2026, including the five books supplied from Downloads. This is a build plan for the private campaign table, not a replacement for the books. Keep the app's 2014 fifth edition profile and its named character exceptions. Published adventures and Echo Below additions must remain visibly distinct on the DM screen.

The app can now link those exact sources after OCR and spot checks. Until a card is verified, mark it as unverified instead of inventing or assuming statistics.

## Build order

### 1. Finish the Phandelver path for the next sessions

Create reusable packs in play order: Phandalin and its NPCs, Redbrand Hideout, the branching Spider's Web leads, Cragmaw Castle, then Wave Echo Cave. Each package should have a DM scene card, keyed room/page link, start positions, enemy and ally cards, objectives, treasure, likely exits, and a private Echo overlay. Preserve the book's location and NPC conflicts. Let play outcomes determine which pack is next.

Add an *LMoP* shelf table of contents: introduction p. 2, Goblin Arrows p. 6, Phandalin p. 14, Spider's Web p. 27, Wave Echo Cave p. 42, magic items p. 52, monsters p. 54, and rules index p. 64. Build a corrected OCR index for this scan and verify map and room page jumps. The current Cragmaw cards can then link to the exact room rather than a broad page range.

### 2. Make the Mad Mage book navigable before building its encounters

Add it as a **DM-only** local shelf book. Link the adventure overview (printed pp. 5-12), the 23 floor chapters (printed pp. 13-289), Skullport (p. 303), dungeon creatures (p. 310), elder runes (p. 318), and secrets (p. 319). Link each existing floor dashboard card directly to its chapter, floor map, and aftermath. Store both printed and PDF page so a jump can be checked and corrected.

Build a route view showing stairs, rivers, portals, gates, Skullport, and known return paths. It should distinguish a discovered route from a possible route and display a gate's book restriction when the DM selects it. The book's first floor is designed for four 5th-level characters; later floor numbers are **not** character levels. Show recommended level as advice beside the party's actual level, with retreat and return available.

### 3. Add floor preparation one or two sessions ahead

For the next likely floor, create a package with the printed map/page link, room index, active factions, wandering encounters, hazards, likely bargains, rewards, and aftermath. The first floor involves the Undertakers and Xanathar-linked occupants; the second has the goblin bazaar and opposing Xanathar interests; the third connects to Skullport. These local conflicts should work even when an Echo clue is absent.

Use the Echo DM book's floor overlay as an optional, clearly labeled cue. Keep published room keys, monsters, treasures, and travel restrictions separate from custom resonance clues. Give the DM a one-click way to mark a clue as observed, inferred, claimed, or still open.

### 4. Extend creatures and combat only from confirmed sources

OCR the *Monster Manual* and build a corrected alphabetical index, then link the exact creature needed by the next encounter. Index *Lost Mine* appendix B and *Mad Mage* appendix A as separate, private, source-linked entries. Check duplicates against the existing SRD goblin, wolf, and bugbear cards before adding variants. Keep an unverified marker until the full stat block and PDF page are checked. Never substitute a vaguely similar stat block without the DM choosing it.

Attach encounter cards to exact room keys. Include surprise, reactions, morale, retreat, negotiation, and environmental triggers. Appendix B elder runes should become an optional DM cue with a private result preview; a rune should never fire merely because a token crosses an area on the map.

### 5. Make maps and secrets safe on the table TV

Prepare clean player maps separately from marked DM book maps. Preserve scale, doors, elevation, and secret areas, and inspect every TV image for printed numbers or concealed labels. Add the book's secret cards and the Echo DM book's six recovered documents to the handout staging area, each with a DM preview and an explicit Show on TV action. Record who has learned each secret so a future recap does not expose undiscovered lore.

### 6. Put the rules beside the action

Turn the existing PHB and DMG references into contextual links: stealth and surprise beside ambushes; light, search, doors, and travel time beside exploration; social interaction beside negotiations; traps and improvised damage beside hazards; downtime and treasure at Phandalin or Waterdeep. Keep automatic calculations narrow and reviewable, especially for cover, spell effects, resistance, and rest recovery.

Add *Xanathar's* as an optional supplement profile. Its downtime and rival procedures suit Waterdeep returns; its trap guidance suits later dungeons; its spell identification and area-on-grid sections belong in the live rules lookup. Add *Catapult* to Donavan's action card with its book/page and current sheet values, without making every optional rule automatic.

Add *Tasha's* as a separately labeled optional supplement. Its group patrons can help organize the Kemp Archive or city factions, and its sidekick framework can support a future follower if the table chooses it. Its supernatural-region and magical-phenomenon ideas can inform Echo scene preparation without rewriting established campaign facts. Link *Mind Sliver* and the future Order of Scribes choice to Donavan, but show the class path as a future choice until he reaches the appropriate level and selects it. Do not merge Tasha's optional Monk or Rogue features into Trista or Ringgo without a character-specific decision.

## Practical limits before relying on the new books

- The scanned PHB is incomplete, and *Lost Mine* has no usable text layer. A search result must identify which book/page was actually checked.
- The *Monster Manual* is now available, but its scan has no text layer and is not indexed in the app. The 406-page PDF includes additional pages after the printed index; confirm offsets against several creature entries before relying on page jumps.
- *Xanathar's* and *Tasha's* are supplements to the selected 2014 profile. Their subclasses, optional class features, and alternate procedures need explicit table choices and a source label.
- The book PDFs and extracted maps are for this private local table. A version distributed to others needs separate content and media licensing review; the shareable SRD can remain the public rules source.
- The existing player guides and campaign book should be checked against the live sheets and what actually happens at the table. A planned clue, relationship, or outcome is not automatically established in play.

## Recommended first implementation slice

Add the *Monster Manual*, *Xanathar's*, and *Tasha's* to the private shelf with verified page links; resolve Donavan's two spell exceptions against their supplied pages. Add the *Mad Mage* floor links and *Lost Mine* chapter index. Then build the **Phandalin arrival** package with its player map or scene art, NPC cards, leads, and private read-aloud. This gives the next session immediate value while establishing the navigation pattern for the rest of the campaign.
