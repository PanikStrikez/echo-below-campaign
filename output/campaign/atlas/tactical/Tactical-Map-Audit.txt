PHANDELVER TACTICAL MAP CORRECTIONS — REVIEW PACKAGE
Date: 2026-10-08

DELIVERY
20 proposed public maps, derived from 6 editable native SVG geometry masters.
19 tactical boards use whole-column 5-foot grid metadata; the Phandalin town
map is for orientation, with an approximate source ruler and no tactical grid.
All proposal scenes retain their legacy scene IDs as identity references.
Nothing in this package registers a scene, changes a selected image, writes a
save, resets fog, or moves tokens. The collection is proposal data only.

SOURCE AND PRECISION
Inspected the local private keyed map scans and relevant printed room keys:
Cragmaw Hideout p9; Phandalin p17; Redbrand p21 / keys20–25; Thundertree p31 /
keys30–34; Cragmaw Castle p37 / keys35–41; Wave Echo p43 / keys42–51.
The private native scans are 1382×1900. Working coordinates subtract (140,110)
from native scan pixels. Anchors can be translated back without resizing.

These are manual, source-reconciled redraws, not pixel-perfect vector traces.
Door centers and room footprints were sampled from the inspected source;
natural walls, rubble boundaries, roofs, vegetation, and some furnishings are
simplified. This limitation is stated in every proposal. Exact-looking grid
numbers describe the authored geometry and do not prove survey accuracy.

Calibrations: Redbrand about39px/5ft; Hideout38.5px/5ft; Castle37.3px/5ft;
Thundertree39.3px/10ft; Wave Echo31.7px/10ft. Phandalin uses about493px/500ft
from the source distance ruler, with tacticalMeasurement:false.
Each tactical view is expanded to an enclosing source-grid-aligned 3:2
rectangle: whole columns/rows, zero offset, no stretching. All public boards
declare an SVG viewport of1536×1024; they are resolution-independent vectors,
not rasters with native pixel detail. The extra Wave Echo master declares1125×1500 and
is a full-volume reference, not an automatically registered battleboard.

REUSE WITH HONEST LIMITS
Conyberry, Triboar Trail, road ambush, night camp, Agatha's grove, Old Owl Well,
Wyvern Tor, Sleeping Giant, Tresendar courtyard and Phandalin street remain
usable as original encounter staging. They have no claim of exact published
keyed geometry. LMOP provides no keyed Conyberry town floorplan to verify;
its invented river/ruin layout is a campaign staging choice. Existing5ft/30-column
grids on such boards describe a chosen encounter space, not surveyed geography.
The cave approach remains original exterior staging and returns to the hideout
mouth by navigation; no unsupported geographic pixel alignment is claimed.
Full existing-scene classifications are in the collection audit array.

PLAYER/DM SEPARATION
Public SVGs contain only native paths, rectangles, circles, groups, and local
paint definitions. There are no text elements, keys, labels, monsters, treasure,
hidden doors, encoded room identifiers, external assets, scripts or source scans.
They do show full ordinary floor layouts: stage privately and use fog before
showing unexplored dungeon areas. Being spoiler-free art does not make a fully
revealed dungeon appropriate for an unexplored session.
The DM companion and generator deliberately contain room names and secrets.
Proposal playerTitle fields supply neutral captions for the ruined tower,
eastern cottage/chamber, workshop and northern hall. The legacy title field
retains the existing DM catalog title; do not expose unknown inhabitants through it.
Current play, discoveries, defeats, ownership, and stopping point are not
inferred from presence of a map, a source key, or an asset filename.

VERIFICATION
Read-only test command: node tools/check-phandelver-map-collection.cjs
620 assertions passed before final handoff, including source/native sizes,
original-art hashes, no public text/raster/scripts/semantic spoilers, whole
aligned grids, identical master geometry across crops, valid return links,
mandatory source connections, and sampled visible openings/hidden wall pixels.
The tests found and helped fix a Smelter join and the store's internal wall.
Full-map renders were inspected visually. These checks are a regression guard,
not proof that every irregular source-wall pixel is matched. Review is still
required for approximate tracing, decorative detail, and any saved-scene move.

ADOPTION
Prepare a separate review board or explicitly migrate after DM review. Keep
legacy scene IDs, selected original art, saved fog, tokens, drawings, notes,
expedition links, and custom art intact. Never reuse old coordinates merely
because a proposal happens to have the same number of grid columns.
