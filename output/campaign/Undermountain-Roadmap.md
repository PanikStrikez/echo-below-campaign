# Undermountain — the long campaign

DM supplement for **The Echo Below** • Spoilers throughout

Use this chapter beside *Waterdeep: Dungeon of the Mad Mage*. It supplies the custom story overlay, pacing, and connections to Waterdeep; the book supplies room keys, maps, stat blocks, treasure, access restrictions, and local inhabitants. Preserve the dungeon's independent conflicts. An Echo clue on every floor would make both the mystery and the dungeon feel smaller.

**Dungeon floor and character level are different numbers.** The prior plan placed floors 1, 2, and 3 at approximately character levels 5, 6, and 7. Later ranges describe broad arcs, not promises of one advancement per floor. Award milestones for substantial accomplishments across dungeon and satellite adventures together. An optional site need not add another level beyond the planned progression.

## I. The Echo Below

**Floors 1–3 and Skullport • characters approximately 5–8.** The party seeks the meaning of what happened beneath Phandalin. Their immediate reward is proof that the mystery reaches Waterdeep. Save the full explanation of the knot for later.

| Floor | Preserve | Add to this campaign |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Dungeon Level** | The dangerous welcome, previous expeditions, and competing inhabitants. | Echo's Whisper gives its paired response. The second pulse is stronger than at Wave Echo Cave: this is closer to the source. Give several witnesses something observable. |
| **2. Arcane Chambers** | Arcane experiments and the dungeon's layered magical history. | A damaged radial diagram bears **RESONANCE OBSERVED**. Its surviving marks match the medallion. Donavan can recognize its disciplined notation as a field record rather than a complete theory, giving him an earned reason to preserve every uncertainty. It proves prior research, without explaining the knot or giving a complete map. |
| **3. Sargauth Level** | River travel, factions, and the route toward Skullport. | Someone recognizes the symbol and asks where they found it, then becomes guarded. Ringgo can recognize the conversational shape of a criminal probe without knowing this organization. Recognition creates a lead; it does not make the stranger an omniscient guide or tie Ringgo's past to the Echo. |

**Skullport** is a substantial underground hub. Mara can meet the party again here after her Waterdeep introduction. Her contacts know that buyers seek black stone artifacts. Ringgo's experience lets him read prices, escape routes, and coded pressure, but Skullport is not automatically controlled by his former employers. Trista can decide whether Mara's access is worth the personal cost; Donavan can identify what testimony would survive scrutiny outside this place. Keep the buyers' motives incomplete. Introduce the Silent Library investigation during a return to Waterdeep; discovering the Nine gives this arc a wider horizon without requiring an immediate world tour.

## II. Halaster Notices

**Floors 4–8 • characters approximately 8–10.** The mystery becomes personal. Evidence that someone watches the expedition should grow gradually, while local adventures and relationships carry most sessions.

| Floor | Preserve | Add to this campaign |
|---|---|---|
| **4. Twisted Caverns** | Environmental strangeness and local inhabitants. | A changed doorway, moved object, or oddly personal message suggests observation. Use one incident, not constant interference that invalidates planning. |
| **5. Wyllowwood** | The startling underground forest and its central relationships. | Make room for character-led conversation: Trista can weigh need against taking, Donavan can study without claiming ownership, and Ringgo can practice entering another community without treating it as hostile territory. This is an intentional pause in Echo exposition, not a new shared-origin reveal. |
| **6. Lost Level** | Melairkyn history. | Records describe dreams, pressure, vibrations, and an urge to dig. The phenomenon predates Halaster. **He found it; he did not create it.** |
| **7. Maddgoth's Castle** | Its independent mystery, danger, and strange scale. | Place a tempting valuable object before Trista with observable consequences of taking it. Let her choose; a flaw is an invitation to roleplay. |
| **8. Slitherswamp** | The strong environmental change and local adventure. | Close the arc with Halaster's brief communication: **“Phandalin. … That was you, wasn't it?”** He knows something they never told him. |

The Drowned Observatory can punctuate this stretch. Its warning shows Halaster's knowledge has limits. Returning home should also produce ordinary pleasures: a familiar meal, a repaired possession, or someone remembering a promise.

## III. The Apprentices

**Floors 9–13 • characters approximately 10–12.** Ask what Halaster discovered. Records, witnesses, and apprentices disagree; separate observation from interpretation when presenting their claims.

| Floor | Preserve | Add to this campaign |
|---|---|---|
| **9. Dweomercore** | The academy and competing magical ambitions. | Introduce **the knot in the Weave** by name. A researcher explains that the second pulse is felt magic. Donavan sees a distorted cousin of the ordered spell notation Favian taught him; the resemblance establishes a question, not that the Kemp Archive created or understands the knot. Other theories remain disputed. |
| **10. Muiral's Gauntlet** | Muiral and the level's existing conflicts. | Fragmentary testimony or records describe Halaster seeking places beyond Undermountain that respond to the knot. |
| **11. Troglodyte Warrens** | Survival, combat, and local faction consequences. | Give the PCs time to use what they learned. No major Echo revelation is required. |
| **12. Maze Level** | Spatial uncertainty and the maze's own dangers. | An impossible response hints that resonance is not purely geographical. The medallion does not become a compass, teleport key, or automatic route finder. |
| **13. Trobriand's Graveyard** | Trobriand's technological and magical legacy. | Reveal **PROJECT LOOKING GLASS**. Halaster was building something. Its purpose remains unknown; the record says it is incomplete. Let Donavan choose whether to send a redacted copy to Dianna and Favian, preserve it locally, or tell the party why he cannot trust an institution with it yet. |

Use this corrected research fragment at floor 13:

> PRIMARY RESONANCE: STABLE
> SECONDARY SITES: 8
> CONNECTION: CONFIRMED
> LOOKING GLASS: INCOMPLETE

There are nine sites total: one primary and eight secondary. The old draft's “secondary sites: 9” is retired. A historical report does not establish every site's present condition. The Dead God's Scar can give a PC a personal reason to question what research costs the people living nearby.

## IV. War for Undermountain

**Floors 14–17 • characters approximately 13–14.** The PCs now have influence. Let earlier alliances, rescues, betrayals, and territorial changes affect their options.

| Floor | Preserve | Add to this campaign |
|---|---|---|
| **14. Arcturiadoom** | The apprentice connection and existing threats. | Discover that Looking Glass was **abandoned**. Its cause remains unresolved. |
| **15. Obstacle Course** | Halaster's elaborate tests and theatrical cruelty. | Repeated patterns suggest he measures the party's reactions, magic, and resistance. Evidence supports this inference; avoid explaining every trap as Echo machinery. |
| **16. Crystal Labyrinth / Stardock** | The change of scope and its githyanki material. | Distance supplies a quiet moment: the medallion stops responding, then pulses upon return. This observation does not establish a universal distance rule. |
| **17. Seadeeps** | Deep faction conflict. | Return earlier consequences: an ally offers passage, a spared opponent helps, or a betrayal costs access. Follow actual play. |

The Frozen Door demonstrates that someone understood and sealed resonance before Halaster. The Walking Tower can yield a Looking Glass fragment. Neither discovery must reveal the amplification finding scheduled for the deeper dungeon.

## V. Truth of the Echo

**Floors 18–22 • characters approximately 15–17.** Answer the central historical question while keeping the proposed solutions contestable.

| Floor | Preserve | Add to this campaign |
|---|---|---|
| **18. Vanrakdoom** | Its Shadowfell identity. | Establish that the knot interacts with planar boundaries. The threat extends beyond ordinary physical distance. |
| **19. Caverns of Ooze** | Bizarre exploration and local danger. | A breather from cosmology. Let competence, survival, and character relationships matter. |
| **20. Runestone Caverns** | The level's existing magical importance. | Recover the decisive research: Looking Glass was designed to **AMPLIFY** the knot's resonance. |
| **21. Terminus Level** | Its own encounters and pressures. | Compare conflicting predictions about simultaneous resonance. Changing magic, weakened boundaries, expansion, and distant control remain theories. |
| **22. Shadowdusk Hold** | The Shadowdusk family's extraplanar interests. | Their warning suggests the project could open something that should remain closed. Their agenda limits their credibility. |

The Broken Star provides practical evidence of planar reach. Waterdeep's scholars can compare discoveries, but conclusions and decisions belong to the party. The Still Hand's strongest argument should be supported by evidence rather than a surprise rule that every investigation helps Halaster.

## VI. The Mad Wizard

**Floor 23: Mad Wizard's Lair • characters approximately 17–20.** Resolve the relationship between the party's choices, the Nine, and Looking Glass. Bring back discoveries from Phandalin, people protected or harmed, components entrusted to allies, and sites deliberately tuned or isolated.

Prepare routes to **destroy, complete, rewrite, or confront**; those actions can overlap. Fighting Halaster does not automatically settle the network. Consult the book for his abilities and rejuvenation rather than improvising a permanent solution.

**Provisional, not established:** Halaster might be unable to distinguish his own ambition from the knot's influence. That is one possible interpretation, not proof that the knot is sentient or the fixed reason Looking Glass was abandoned. Leave the Hollow Mountain's relationship to the party and the final outcome responsive to actual play.

Before each expedition, prepare the next one or two sessions in detail. Keep this roadmap broad enough to survive a skipped floor, an unexpected alliance, or a decision to investigate a satellite first.
