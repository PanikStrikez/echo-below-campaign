# Current app status

Open **Menu → Settings → Recovery & campaigns → Current status**.

This is the single current status view. It uses approved outcomes from the selected campaign, dated release evidence, app and asset fingerprints, and the current save scope. Historical reports never establish arrivals, discoveries, or current character statistics.

- **Automated check passed:** isolated checks for matching app and asset versions. Verify current files in the local desktop launcher; file-based opening cannot compare them.
- **DM reviewed:** your explicit dated material review for this campaign and save.
- **Physical table tested:** your explicit observation on the real TV, phones, or speakers.
- **Not checked:** no current matching evidence, expired observation, changed files, or missing check. A simulated browser is not a physical test.

The current view lists changes, remaining checks, and searchable historical reports. A comparison expires after 15 minutes; recheck after updating files. DM observations expire after 24 hours, physical observations after 15 minutes; campaign/save/build/display changes make prior observations historical. Review notes are local to this browser and campaign and do not certify a restored package or another machine.

The previous [9 October baseline](readiness/history/App-Readiness-Status-2026-10-09.md) is preserved as history. Old roadmaps remain future planning. Do not use either as current play records.

Release checks write a dated report and rebuild the evidence index. In the app, **Verify current files** compares those versions without opening books, saves, databases, or provider accounts. If the already-running desktop service predates this check, reopen the desktop launcher when convenient. Phone access is a separate service and was not restarted for this update.

## Physical table rehearsal

Use **Current status → Guided table rehearsal** for an 11-step, 15–20 minute exercise with 38 separate device-step results, including all five phones. The existing practice campaign isolates game changes; opening it releases phone joins and requires its QR. Rehearsal notes belong to the source group, so they remain accessible after returning to the real campaign. **Resume this practice copy** reopens an unfinished rehearsal. Notes can be exported separately; they do not certify another machine or change the campaign history.

Record the observer, real setup, device results, hesitation/unreadable controls, and optional time taken. Simulations are explicitly separate. A prepared fix stays unresolved until a later physical pass; missing or skipped checks remain visible. Saving/Saved messages confirm persistence. A [printable guide](Table-Rehearsal-Guide.html) is also available. Actual physical results have not been supplied for this update.
