# Bird’s-eye town exploration maps

The campaign table includes player-safe, original overhead maps for **Phandalin**, **Conyberry**, **Thundertree**, and **Waterdeep**. Conyberry and Thundertree are abandoned settlements; Waterdeep is a schematic city view. The maps are for exploration and orientation, not measured tactical movement. Use the separate battle maps when exact distances matter.

[Preview all four maps](town-maps/town-map-previews.png).

## At the table

1. Choose **Town map · Phandalin**, **Town map · Conyberry**, **Town map · Thundertree**, or **City map · Waterdeep** from **Screen and map → What the players see**. Session 2 also has **Open Phandalin town map** beside its arrival and town beats.
2. Open **Maps & exploration → Explore** on the DM screen to review each marker. A marker appears on the TV only when **Show marker** and **Approve player interaction** are both on.
3. Players can click a glowing star to see its player-safe clue on the TV. The clue appears over the map; use **Hide handout** in the DM controls when finished.
4. Drag hero tokens to show where the party is moving. The map’s **Player view** control can disable player movement and clicks for a narrated scene.
5. Use **Maps & exploration → Scene** to pan, zoom, and return to the full map.

Hidden markers begin with both controls off: the hillside tunnel at Phandalin, the forest trail at Conyberry, and the custom Echo Below locations in Waterdeep. Thundertree labels buildings without naming their occupants or creatures. Private marker notes stay on the DM screen.

The illustrations and marker positions are approximate campaign aids. Phandalin and Thundertree follow the relative layout of the 2014 adventure’s DM references; Conyberry’s unkeyed landmarks and Waterdeep’s street layout are original schematics.
