# Session 1 visual plan - Gundren's road

*DM-facing presentation guide for a 3-4 hour opening with Trista, Donavan, Ringgo, and Pebble, and Aldric Vane. Use with the [session-one run sheet](Session-01-DM-Run-Sheet.md).*

The [Session 1 image guide](Session-01-Image-Guide.md) labels every player-facing illustration, including the trail, discovered traps, all eight hideout areas, the revealed ambush, and the optional Phandalin branch. It gives the app location and reveal timing for each image.

The scene illustrations set mood; the road, cave approach, and [clean hideout TV map](session-01-visuals/cragmaw-hideout-painted-gridless.png) are painted top-down staging spaces in the same earthy style. The hideout TV map keeps the major room connections without printed room numbers or DM notes; consult the [private source map](session-01-visuals/cragmaw-hideout-map-source-page.png) for exact geometry, room keys, encounter rosters, and traps. The route diagram is a flow map for the DM, not literal regional geography. The scene art likewise does not settle exact weather, distance to Phandalin, or clothing details.

The times are pacing cues, not a timetable. If play is slow, end on the cave reveal; if it is fast, continue into the hideout. Do not force a rescue before the session ends.

## Battle-map settings

The three painted map images are **1536 × 1024** and fill the app's **30 × 20 square** stage. Switch the app's grid on for **5 feet per square**; the grid is drawn separately so the same painted map also works without it. The maps have no enemy markers or printed room keys. The painted hideout layout is an interpretation; use the private adventure map when an exact ledge, wall, or passage width matters.

**Road ambush:** West is the wagon side; the horses lie farther east. The broad road is open movement, while tree trunks, boulders, and brush suggest cover or concealment where the actual line of sight supports it. Do not treat every green patch as a wall. If the wagon is positioned differently in play, place its token where the players established it and treat the painted wagon as set dressing.

**Cave approach:** The stream comes out of the eastern cave mouth, with a southern path through the ravine. The water, bank, stones, and trees provide staging for choices about approach and withdrawal. Use your published adventure's water and sentry mechanics; this exterior art supplies positions, not new hazards or an alternate interior layout.

## DM presentation rules

- Keep the **route schematic private**. It names the cave and hideout before the characters may know them.
- Do not show the ambush battle map until the horses have been discovered and combat positioning matters. The scene illustration carries the discovery moment.
- For the cave, show the illustration before the battle map. The illustration builds uncertainty; the map appears only if movement needs exact measurement.
- Dracuseth is absent from the opening art and Session 1 party. Let the five PCs find clues, choose the route, and decide whether to negotiate, retreat, or press on.
- Keep the official creature counts for the first run. His level-4 durability is not a reason to increase damage against level-1 PCs. See the [opening balance guide](Opening-Encounter-Balance.md).
