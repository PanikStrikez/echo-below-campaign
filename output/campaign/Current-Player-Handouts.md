Pebble’s current level-4 sheet includes the Goron homebrew approved 9 October 2026: **Fire-Forged** (fire resistance; halve damage, round down) and **Rock Eater** (nonmagical stone meets food needs, water still required; eating a magical stone stores its magical properties for 24 in-game hours, during which Pebble chooses when to activate or use its power; the properties expire 24 hours after eating and late activation does not reset expiry. The DM defines manifestation, activation/action cost, and stone-specific uses or limits). No magical stone has been consumed as part of this update.
