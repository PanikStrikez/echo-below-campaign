THE ECHO BELOW — SCENE ASSISTANCE AND NPC DIALOGUE
8 October 2026

WHERE TO FIND IT
Refresh the DM app. Choose Session > DM assistant. It opens in the existing
right panel while the map remains visible. NPC cards also have Dialogue suggestions.

LOCAL ASSISTANCE
Choose run scene, NPC knowledge, clues, rule, next action, or campaign history.
Use campaign records gathers source-linked excerpts, current party statistics,
objectives, preparation and unresolved decisions. Expand a source to read its
status and provenance, review the original outcome, or open a local book page.
Preparation is not proof that an event happened. Unknown answers remain unknown.
Rules use the 2014 base with recorded character exceptions. Skill bonuses and
passive scores use the existing character calculator; saved attacks remain saved
values. Book summaries retain their verification warnings. Books remain local.

NPC DIALOGUE
Choose the NPC, everyone who can hear, and the conversational approach. Enter the
player's words, then draft responses. Local drafts use only approved knowledge
that has been explicitly permitted for every listener. Being known to the party
does not give an NPC that knowledge. Claims and suspicions retain qualifiers.
The private knowledge section shows withheld records; you can withhold more.
Prepared goals, speaking cues and approved relationship/promise records remain
in the DM panel. The local alternatives are conservative templates, not AI prose.

Edit a response and perform it yourself, or check its table-audience review box
and send it to Voice Studio. This does not generate, approve or play any audio.
Private conversations cannot be sent to table narration. New promises, prices,
revelations or relationship changes go through Review consequences first. This
creates a private, planned outcome draft; it does not establish an actual event.
Use the existing outcome review to record what actually happens, correct it or
undo it. Assistant suggestions never change HP, resources, quests or inventory.
Changes to source records invalidate old answers and block stale handoffs.

OPTIONAL PROVIDER DRAFTS
Expand Optional AI draft, review the exact outgoing context, approve that context
and question, then choose Ask configured AI. It uses the existing provider,
credentials, allowance and spending controls. The app remains useful with AI off.
NPC requests omit private motives and withheld knowledge. Local book summaries,
feature text and mechanical calculations are not sent for model reinterpretation.
Context is bounded; it is not the whole campaign archive. Ask focused questions.
Provider output must cite identifiers present in its request. Valid citations do
not prove the interpretation is correct: every AI answer is an unverified draft.
Cancellation is best effort after dispatch; a provider may still count usage.
Closing the panel cancels a pending request. No credentials are stored in these
panel drafts or added to campaign exports.

NARRATION
Use Prepare narration on a runbook opening, or select an approved handout, letter
or published recap in Voice Studio. Generate from the exact selected audience
text, preview privately, approve a take, then explicitly play it to the table.
The existing transport supports pause/resume, skip and stop. Music ducks during
narration without lowering the voice; the prior mix returns afterward. Visual and
ambience staging is explicit. Subtitle-only and ordinary manual reading remain
available. Recap audio becomes stale when approved source events change.
Approved letters now close the story drawer when opening Voice Studio.

VERIFICATION
Passed nine pure assistant checks plus existing audience and voice tests:
confirmed versus unplayed history; superseded records; private audience boundaries;
friendly, deceptive, negotiating and unknown dialogue; beliefs and suspicions;
Trista and Donavan exceptions; existing skill/passive calculations; forged citations;
credential redaction; private planned proposals; no source mutation.
An isolated practice browser rehearsed the right panel, focused actions, context
approval, mock service completion/cancellation, edited wording handoff, stale-record
blocking, private conversation blocking, outcome drafts and a separate TV view.
Narration rehearsal used five simulated requests and synthetic audio: exact scene
text, private preview, approval, pause/resume/skip/stop, music ducking, subtitle-only,
recap invalidation, offline replay, missing-device safety and backup restoration.
Ten sessions reopened; four audio files were verified byte-for-byte after restore.
Map, tokens and combat did not change from assistant use or narration.

LIMITATIONS
No real text or speech provider request was made for this implementation. Mock
transport is not generated prose, and synthetic audio is not an audible voice test.
Existing provider accounts/limits were not changed. Test real headphones, speakers,
TV and phones at the table. Keep reviewed text/manual reading available if AI or
audio is unavailable. Live phone access was not restarted or reconfigured.
