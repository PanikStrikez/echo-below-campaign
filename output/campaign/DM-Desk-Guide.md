# A simpler live DM workspace

Refresh the DM app to load these controls. No phone server restart is needed for this update.

## During play

- **Right-click a map token** for its combat card, damage/healing review, targeting, movement path, reaction toggle, visibility, TV ping or character sheet. Keyboard: focus a token and press **Shift+F10**; Enter opens its card. The card also has **Quick actions**.
- **Inbox** at the top opens the sidebar queue: phone actions, exploration and inventory requests, saves, reactions, staged damage, due effects, expired clocks, initiative collection, town deadlines and your unresolved decisions. Review opens the relevant existing tool. Items from other scenes remain listed; return to that scene before resolving them when prompted.
- **Workspace** selects Exploration, Conversation or Combat. These adjust the DM layout and default sidebar. To remember open panels and a sidebar tab, use **More tools → Session desk → Workspaces → Save current arrangement**. Desktop widths change only on wide screens; smaller screens keep the responsive layout.
- Search now boosts the current scene, current beats, selected encounter and party. Empty search shows contextual suggestions. Search also finds improvised NPC notes, clues and locations.
- Specialist controls now live in **More tools**, keeping the main toolbar shorter.

## Pacing and improvisation

**More tools → Session desk → Pacing** shows the current and next prepared beat, party objective, your session objective, a planned finish time, suggested stopping beats and where to resume. Suggestions do not advance the story. Add unresolved decisions here so they stay in the Inbox.

**Keep an improvised detail** creates a private permanent NPC, Open clue/evidence record, or Location journal entry. You can copy your scratchpad first. These records remain available in Story and Search across sessions. Publishing remains a separate decision.

## Closing the session

Open **Session desk → Close session**. It gathers an editable private draft of pending requests, due reminders, unfinished travel/combat, open tracked commitments, expedition work, party HP/conditions and recent table changes. Use **Gather / refresh** if play continued after you opened it.

**Save review & recovery snapshot** first creates a campaign recovery point, then saves your review. It does not finish combat, spend resources, resolve decisions, rest characters or publish a recap. The campaign recap editor is available beside this workflow for your approved summary.

The Inbox is derived from the existing records; it does not maintain a second approval queue. Workspace choices, pacing and closing reviews are saved with the campaign and excluded from player TV/phone projections.

## Verification

Automated checks cover Inbox status filtering and stale requests, token-menu dispatch, pacing, review capture, canonical record creation, persistence and Undo/Redo, TV/phone privacy, contextual search, existing phone actions, combat tools, table workflows and settlement review. A live visual rehearsal on the actual TV and phones was not performed for this update.
