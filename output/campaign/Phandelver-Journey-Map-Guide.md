# Phandelver journey map

Reload the DM app and player TV, then press **Journey map** in the live controls. The painted terrain matches the campaign’s existing town and battle maps.

Click a star on the DM map to open its location card. Reveal a location and approve interaction when the party learns where it is. Players see only approved locations; the DM’s notes stay private. Use **Show detailed map on TV** to enter a prepared town or encounter map.

The **Animate journey map** checkbox at the top of the location panel turns the coastal shimmer, woodland mist, and known-location glow on or off. **Reduce animation on both screens** in the app’s comfort settings, and the device’s reduced-motion preference, also stop these effects. The map itself stays stationary and fully opaque.

Existing saves automatically replace the original schematic with the painting. Untouched original marker positions move onto the new landmarks once. Edited notes, revealed locations, manually moved markers, zoom, and pan are preserved. A custom replacement image is preserved.

The [painted image](town-maps/professional/phandelver-region.png) is original custom campaign art created with the built-in image generation tool. The [full generation prompt](town-maps/Phandelver-Journey-Map-Prompt.md) is included. This is an approximate journey overview, not a measured travel map or an officially published map. The original schematic remains in `town-maps/phandelver-region.svg`.

When moving the app to another computer, copy its artwork folders as well as restoring the campaign save.
