# Intervention and finale framework

DM preparation · current five-character campaign · future possibilities, not actual events

Use **World → Intervention & finale** in the app for the playable scene cards, prerequisite reviews, alternatives and epilogue prompts. This follows **Echo-Below-Campaign-Outline.md, Act V**, replacing this file’s older four-route proposal and three-character ending. The previous version is preserved in `archive/finale-before-2026-10-01/Finale.md`.

## Start from actual evidence

Latest confirmed play ended at Barthen’s Provisions after the party left Gundren at an unnamed inn and Trista kept Glasstaff’s captured rat. None of these finale preparations, evacuations or technical successes has happened merely because a card exists.

Prepare three briefs before commitment:

1. **Current activation plan:** observed preparations, sources, missing materials or access, and warnings before the next step.
2. **People and places at risk:** apparatus inspection, independent city observations, uncertain boundaries, resident decisions and checked routes.
3. **Tested limits:** the actual result of each method, witnesses, consent, stop conditions, failures and what cannot be extrapolated.

Record actual discoveries and choices through Outcome review. The finale desk cites those records; it does not create a competing history. Corrections or withdrawn sources require fresh review. Claims, interpretations, quest completion and multiple copies of one account do not prove a method. Record which characters know each fact; private DM knowledge is not party knowledge.

## Five compatible approaches

| Approach | What needs support | Costs and unresolved consequences |
|---|---|---|
| **Stop the apparatus** | Reachable interruption points, inspection and a bounded shutdown method | Protect residents, isolate unstable remnants and arrange continued watch. Stopping one component does not prove the whole apparatus safe. |
| **Persuade Halaster** | Credible evidence, dialogue and an observable commitment | He may delay, refuse or change his mind. Verify his actions; sympathy does not erase accountability. |
| **Attempt a limited rescue** | A bounded method, independent safeguards, willing participants and a stop condition | Contact or embodiment may fail, identity may remain uncertain and any returned person has independent wishes. Aldric owes no participation. |
| **Protect people during activation** | Checked routes, warnings, resident decisions and reachable controls | Prevention can fail while rescue succeeds. A blocked route needs another inspected exit; casualties are never automatic. |
| **Preserve a tested discovery** | One demonstrated limited application, willing custodians, monitoring and a way to stop it | Preserve failed tests and limits alongside benefits. Custody, maintenance and access remain practical responsibilities. |

Choose a primary approach and compatible fallbacks. No satellite is compulsory: witnesses, independent dungeon records, local trials and rival expeditions can also supply bounded methods. Partial successes count. Stopping expansion, evacuating residents and preserving a harmless measurement can coexist.

## Run the decision

Scene cards cover a corroborated warning, resident meeting, route walk, apparatus inspection, negotiations, intervention and return. Stage only what actual access and play support. No preparation button starts activation, advances a deadline, damages a resident or spends resources.

A future activation requires observable preparation and a stated trigger. Refusal does not instantly cause catastrophe. Inspect the actual floor, published room key, hazards and inhabitants before placing the custom apparatus overlay. The framework creates no city-to-dungeon portal.

Residents decide whether to leave, help, stay elsewhere or request assistance. The party cannot consent for the district. Proposed refuges and routes need permission, inspection, capacity and a return arrangement; friendly NPCs and businesses are not automatically volunteered.

Use apparatus objectives and encounter branches for negotiation, engineering, protection, retreat, combat or a combination. Describe the physical objective and risk before a roll. New objective mechanics are original campaign suggestions; published local content remains authoritative.

## Separate Halaster from the apparatus

Use **Dungeon of the Mad Mage, printed p. 310** (local PDF p. 311). Halaster’s published Rejuvenation applies if he dies in Undermountain: after **1d10 days**, he returns in a new body with full hit points at a randomly determined safe location in Undermountain. Record the actual death location and timing before applying it. A disappearance or apparent defeat does not establish death.

Shutting down Looking Glass does not cancel that trait. Defeating Halaster does not shut down Looking Glass or establish district safety. The conditional trait does not itself establish guaranteed permanent death elsewhere. Destroying the knot is not a presumed available method.

Keep separate actual outcomes for the apparatus, Halaster, residents, rescue, knowledge and custody. Completing one does not complete the others.

## Hollow Mountain remains optional

The Ninth site’s identity, exact location and relationship to player-created connections remain open. It is not a predetermined finale arena. Do not require a magical mark to change. If actual choices provide a meaningful lead, prepare its people, route and limits then; its contribution may complement an intervention without becoming a universal solution.

## Epilogues from the ledger

Link approved actual outcomes to epilogue subjects in the app. Prompts ask what follows; they do not choose forgiveness, restoration, punishment, retirement or a happy ending. Stale and speculative records remain visibly unresolved. Any published recap still needs an audience and wording review.

- **Mending Court and Waterdeep:** who returned, repairs, promises and residents’ next decisions.
- **Phandalin:** which actual survivors and custodians receive a truthful message about the mine and discoveries.
- **Trista:** custody of dangerous discoveries and the relationships she chooses to trust.
- **Pebble:** what he tells the hammer-makers, the routes he can explain and where he chooses to go.
- **Donavan:** what he shares with Dianna or the Kemp Archive, credit and remaining uncertainty.
- **Ringgo:** which work and relationships he continues, and what remains his to resolve.
- **Aldric:** corroborated memories, meaningful atonement and a future independent of Halaster.

Give each player the last choice about their character’s future. End with the lives they actually affected, allowing unresolved questions to remain.
