Private next-turn drafts — verified 14 October 2026

Find the workflow
Phone action card -> Prepare next turn -> choose action/targets and intent.
Save draft for later parks it; Resume prepared action restores it.
Cancel private draft removes local intent only. Existing submitted requests retain
revision and cancellation controls. Submit explicitly on the player's turn.
Reaction requests keep their explicit out-of-turn path.

Behavior
One private prepared action is stored locally per phone origin, current QR join
and character. It survives ordinary updates, page reload and Wi-Fi reconnect.
It is excluded from DM state, player transport, campaign saves and backups until
explicit submission. Drafting does not reserve resources, roll dice, change HP,
resolve effects, move tokens, or create a pending request.
Fresh state is fetched before submission; changed character, scene, action details,
visibility, preparation, resources, casting pool and current turn are checked.
Conflicts keep intent editable and show a correction. Missing targets are removed
only by a deliberate action. Current target choices refresh without replacing the
note field. Updated mechanics require an explicit current-action/scene refresh.
Casting exceptions and range/cover/other situational legality remain DM review.
After submission, the same existing action review handles effects and spending.
No automatic submission occurs at turn start or reconnect.
Storage failures are visible; keep the tab open when local storage is unavailable.
A new QR/table or another character uses separate storage and does not inherit a
previous character's private draft. Private drafts are local device conveniences,
not canonical history or portable campaign records.

Verified results
25 unit and mechanics regression tests passed (5 draft checks, 20 existing card/
party checks), plus the existing composer/request regression.
8 browser checks passed in fresh Chrome profiles with disposable local services:
private waiting draft with zero action traffic; reload with targets/intent intact;
offline editing and reconnect; resource/hidden-target conflicts; corrected explicit
submission on the player's turn with sheets, rolls and tokens unchanged; local
cancellation; service rejection of changed mechanics before enqueue; service
resource recheck. Screenshots captured at 360 and 390 pixels without overflow.
Player phone and DM ran entirely in isolated stores. Real saves, maps, characters,
phone assignments, source books and live services were not modified by testing.
JavaScript and phone-service syntax checks passed.

Deployment and limitations
Reload phone pages to load local drafting. No live phone service was restarted.
The added service-side stale-entry/race check loads at its next normal restart;
client fresh-state checks and existing DM review work with the running service.
Real phones and table readability were not physically tested.
Drafts survive connection drops, not deleting browser storage or changing the QR.
