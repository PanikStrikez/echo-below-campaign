# Phone actions: one DM review

## Start using it

Refresh the DM campaign app. Open **Player phones**, use the current QR code, and have each player choose their character. Existing phone tabs should also refresh. The companion will ask for a DM refresh if the old DM screen is still running.

## Player flow

1. In **My turn / Table**, choose **Request action**, **Ask to cast**, or **Ask to use**.
2. Select the visible targets, including yourself when appropriate. For a location, object, or utility effect without a token, explain it in the note.
3. Choose normal / advantage / disadvantage and add relevant details: spell level, dart allocation, area, or intent.
4. Send the request. Its card shows **waiting**, **needs clarification**, **resolved**, or **declined**. Approval and resolution are recorded separately in its history but applied together.
5. While waiting or needing clarification, use **Revise** or **Cancel request**. Once resolved, see your rolls and result on the same card. **Quick roll only** remains a standalone roll; it does not apply damage or spend resources.

Requests remain available outside your turn for reactions and exploration. The DM must explain an override before using an action or bonus action out of turn during combat.

## DM flow

Open **Actions** in the right sidebar, or **Review** beside a phone request.

The review contains the player's intent and targets, action usage, resource changes, concentration, and effect steps. Recognized action cards supply initial values. For a spell whose complete mechanics are not on its card, fill in the slot, timing, and effect explicitly. A levelled spell needs a resource cost or a written free-casting/ritual ruling.

- **Attack:** target, attack modifier, advantage, cover, critical range, damage dice/type.
- **Save:** ability, DC, roll mode, damage on success, and any condition on failure. Save bonuses come from the current character/creature card.
- **Healing / damage / effect:** direct amounts, dice, conditions, or an AC change.
- **Multiple attacks, darts, or targets:** add or duplicate steps and set each recipient. Use the same **Shared dice group** on steps that must reuse one damage roll, such as a single area spell. Use separate steps/groups for independently rolled attacks.
- **Concentration:** keep, replace, or end the acting character's concentration. Damage to a concentrating target also previews its Constitution save; choose its save mode when applicable.
- **Costs:** negative numbers spend, positive numbers restore. A removed listed cost requires a written override. Explicitly select slot level and any extra costs such as ammunition.

Check the review box and choose **Prepare rolls & preview**. Nothing is spent yet. The preview shows rolls, hit/save outcomes, damage after defenses and temporary HP, resources, concentration, and action usage. Choose **Approve & resolve together** to save all reviewed changes in one operation.

The DM can choose **Needs clarification** with a message or **Decline** instead. A changed scene, target, sheet, combat value, or request invalidates a prepared preview; prepare again after reviewing the change.

## Corrections and history

**Actions → History → Undo this resolution** reverses its tracked changes and removes its rolls. The phone then says **undone**. If a touched value has changed since resolution, the app refuses to overwrite the later change: use the main session **Undo** to reverse intervening changes first. The regular Undo/Redo system also includes request changes and resolutions.

Requests and their private resolution records are stored in session saves/backups. The retained history is the most recent 200 requests; the sidebar shows the latest 60 completed entries and a phone the latest 40 of its own entries.

## DM judgment still needed

This is a reviewed action workflow, not a complete spell rules engine. Confirm range, line of sight, action counts, conditional defenses, special features, and duration. Complex spell allocations are explicit steps; special ongoing effects and later expiry belong in the effect note and existing combat reminders. Conditions, AC changes, and concentration markers do not automatically remove every other effect created by an earlier spell. Enemy saving-throw and concentration rolls stay private; the owner receives their own approved attack/effect rolls and reviewed outcome. No private resolution plan or target statistics are sent to the TV or phones.
