WATERDEEP AND SETTLEMENT ATLAS — SOURCE AND VISUAL REVIEW
8 October 2026 · DM-only preparation

SOURCE GEOMETRY
Waterdeep: Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide printed p56, supplied PDF57, rendered
1190×1550. Map frame cropped [28,67,1095,1357] to
  tmp/waterdeep-audit/Waterdeep-Reference.png
This 1095×1357 north-up frame is the city coordinate authority. City coastline,
mountain, cemetery, wards, Market, major road and wall lines were manually
sampled/generalized. The eastern title/crest field is replaced by illustrative
Undercliff grass in the new painting; that space was not a surveyed terrain map.

Dragon Heist supplied 219-page scan lacks the city poster foldout. Its contents,
low-text/map candidates and end material were checked. It is not treated as a
complete reproduction. The ward chapter was rendered and the relevant printed
pages visually read: 176/PDF170 (Castle/North and temples), 177/PDF171 (Portal
history/Trades), 178/PDF172 (Trades/Southern/Dock), 179/PDF173 (City of Dead),
180/PDF174 (Field Ward, Undercliff, Undermountain). The latter confirms Field Ward
is not an official ward. No universal Dragon Heist page offset was used.
SCAG printed54/PDF55 ward descriptions were also visually inspected.

Yawning Portal is a published Castle Ward venue. The supplied city map does not
label its building; the atlas working point is an approximate ward anchor. It is
not a source-verified street address. The new tavern floor is separately audited
in tactical/Waterdeep-Map-Audit.txt and its collection, with original room
geometry and a source-informed well; no full published floorplan tracing claimed.

Custom businesses: Second Margin and Reed & Ledger use the hub's Castle Ward
context; Ninepin Works uses Trades Ward; Lantern House uses Southern Ward; Blue
Lantern Quay uses Dock Ward. All exact blocks are proposals. Mending Court is
proposed near the Portal in Castle Ward for atlas preparation only: the approved
courtyard plan still leaves actual ward, street, distance and compass bearing
unassigned. No rental, keys, access or relationship is established by this map.

LEILON FUTURE STOP
Campaign-Transition-Data.js proposes Phandalin → Triboar Trail west → High Road
south → Waterdeep. Leilon is a plausible optional stop, not a mandated/visited
stop. Its regional placement is sourced in Geography-Register.json. The supplied
material does not provide a verified Leilon street plan. The local road yard,
wayfarers' lodge, supply shed, repair yard and roadside shrine are five original
proposals. No published businesses or NPCs, available inventory, spell services,
prices, room agreements or completed purchases are asserted. All five records
start hidden. Service conversations and continuing north/south are prepared;
actual availability, timing and route decisions remain in play.

RENDER REVIEW
The corrected Waterdeep painting, all six DM overview exports, and the five
vector diagrams were rendered and visually inspected. City harbor, cemetery,
mountain and corrected castle fit the source frame at the intended broad scale.
Minor street/roof positions and wall curves drift. Ward boundaries are broad
relationships, not legal/surveyed boundaries. Marker leader lines preserve source
anchors. City labels fit without clipped names. New venue labels explicitly say
proposal in private exports; player art contains no custom venue labels or keys.
The northern inner-wall feature is generalized and not a measured wall trace.
The four lighter city/town overview vectors are sparse source-relative diagrams;
their roof texture is decorative, not a claim of mapped individual buildings.

SCOPE
Selected maps/pages and campaign preparation were reviewed, not every page of
all PDFs. No GPS accuracy, miles-per-pixel, exact street plan or whole-city battle
scale is claimed. SVG dimensions are coordinate viewports, not native raster
pixels. No discovery, arrival, tenancy, dungeon reset or live service restart was
performed by this data/build work. Source books and old images remain unchanged.

LEILON PAINTED OVERVIEW REVIEW
New terrain is 1536×1024 native raster pixels in a 1200×800 coordinate frame.
The proposed lodge, supply building, repair yard, shrine and road yard markers
were aligned to the actual painted features and the final DM export inspected.
The original SVG diagram remains an alternate board with its own schematic
coordinates; it is not described as a crop of or exact match to the painting.

WATERDEEP PRIVATE ROOM OVERLAYS
Fourteen DM annotated exports use room bounds and entrance points from the
private authored topology. Labels are private metadata (public:false and
sourceApproved:false) and never baked into public SVGs. Crop coordinates are
normalized only in portable exports; the live atlas keeps original master-space
viewBox values. Public image review remains distinct from permission/tenancy.

FINAL CROP REVIEW
Final court, shared, lodging and bindery DM exports were rendered at 1200 pixels
wide and visually inspected. Lodging labels are short, separated and inside
their rooms; the shared landing, well and table are labelled. Bindery retains
its correct east-side footprint. Nonzero crop origins are subtracted only for
portable SVG export; app labelPlacements are translated back to master space.
The Waterdeep collection reports 717 checks for 14 approved views/eight masters.
