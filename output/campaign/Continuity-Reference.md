## Settled backbone

- Title: **The Echo Below**. *Lost Mine of Phandelver* → Waterdeep → *Dungeon of the Mad Mage*. Start at character level 1, use milestones, expect approximately level 20 at the conclusion.
- Waterdeep is home. The Yawning Portal is the practical base. Undermountain supplies the central mystery; the satellites provide reasons to return to the wider world.
- The BOOK knot in the Weave beneath Waterdeep is the foundation. The custom **Echo** is the resonance produced by that knot, not a second competing supernatural force or another knot in Wave Echo Cave.
- Nine total sites: **Undermountain in the center, eight satellites**. Not ten sites. Not twenty-four sites. Not twenty-three Echo sites or twenty-three medallion spokes.
- The sites resonate with the Prime Echo. They are **not a portal network**. Identifying a site does not provide transportation to it.
- Halaster has studied/mapped resonance for centuries but has not visited every site. He did not create Wave Echo Cave, is not responsible for every PC's suffering, and did not order Nezznar's campaign.
- **Nezznar acts independently.** His public goal remains Wave Echo Cave and the Forge of Spells; his private research suggests a greater magical discovery beneath Waterdeep.
- Halaster does not know the PCs at campaign start. An interaction at Wave Echo Cave causes a reaction in Undermountain that brings them to his attention. Its exact player-chosen trigger should be prepared flexibly.
- Early sessions should still feel like *Lost Mine*. Do not disclose Halaster, the knot, all Nine, or Looking Glass immediately.
- A paired Echo motif is **BOOM … boom**: cave surf or another physical event, then a quieter pulse of magical pressure. The second is not literally a second ordinary sound. Away from cave surf, describe pressure rather than implying every echo has a wave source.

### Looking Glass: what is true versus open

**Established:** Halaster researched the knot, mapped responsive sites, started Looking Glass, left it incomplete, abandoned it, and intended amplification. His renewed interest in the party follows their Wave Echo interaction. Different apprentices and factions hold different theories. The Walking Tower contains a fragment. The Broken Star expands the perceived planar scope.

**Open:** why it was abandoned; what completing it actually does; whether the knot has any agency; the exact relationship between Halaster's obsession and his wishes; what methods permit safe rewriting or permanent severance; the finale.

The earlier floor 23 suggestion—Halaster cannot tell his own wish to complete the project from the knot's supposed urge to propagate—was explicitly **PROVISIONAL**. Never silently promote that to settled truth or turn the knot into a confirmed sentient villain.

The three competing interests are the party seeking understanding, Halaster seeking completion, and an anti-network group seeking destruction/severance. The group's warning that exploration is finishing his experiment is an argument to test, not a law that every act of study empowers him.
