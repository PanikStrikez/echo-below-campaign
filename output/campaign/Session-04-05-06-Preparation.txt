SESSION 4 — PHANDALIN AFTER GLASSTAFF
Aim: settle the immediate consequences and let the party choose a useful next inquiry.
1. Opening outside Barthen: check current sheets, devices and backup; ask for a short recap, then invite their choice.
2. Three available leads, any order: supplies/business at Barthen; check Gundren’s actual condition; examine or decide what to do with Trista’s rat.
3. If pursuing the escape: use available Redbrand witnesses, relevant records or Qelline’s lead to Reidoth. A Black Spider letter is not a road map. A route to the castle does not establish Glasstaff’s whereabouts.
4. If returning to the hideout: resume the saved expedition. Establish who remains; do not respawn prisoners, enemies, treasure or traps.
5. If they decline: town services, legitimate work, confirmed rewards not yet paid, or preparation for a different established lead remain playable.
Stop: a departure decision, safe town rest or unresolved inquiry. Review actual discoveries, promises, custody and audience permissions.
Suggested pacing: 10-minute opening, 45–75 town decisions, 45–60 inquiry/local scene, 15-minute closing. Adapt to the table.

SESSION 5 — LEADS AND CRAGMAW CASTLE
Aim: follow an earned route, explore the castle and determine what its inhabitants actually do.
Entry condition: an established route and a player decision to follow it. Otherwise continue inquiry or optional Thundertree; a calendar number does not force travel.
1. Choose purpose, supplies, marching order and retreat conditions.
2. Regional approach and scouting. Enter by the chosen accessible route, following published connectivity.
3. Use the complete castle room guide and linked encounters; optional Thundertree room cards remain available if Reidoth is their chosen lead.
4. Grol, Snarl and Vyerith retain their source roles. Gundren is not their hostage in this campaign’s current situation.
5. Privately stage Glasstaff’s refuge: negotiated protection, watched holding space or concealed escape preparation. His precise room and remaining resources are not yet established. Review the existing evil-mage card before adding a token; do not grant him fresh spells or staff charges automatically.
6. Negotiation, capture, surrender, bypass and retreat are valid. The rat cannot automatically track its master. Castle information may lead toward the mine only when earned and recorded.
Stop: a safe withdrawal, negotiated agreement, unresolved occupied room or reviewed mine-route discovery.
Suggested pacing: 20–40 route/travel, 20–30 scouting, 90–120 connected scenes, 15 closing. Thundertree may become a separate evening.

SESSION 6 — WAVE ECHO CAVE EXPEDITION
Aim: pursue a chosen mine objective while maintaining a credible return route.
Entry condition: earned mine directions or map custody, a decision to enter, and a resource review. Continue the castle or earlier lead if those conditions are unmet.
1. Prepare lights, supplies, marching order and withdrawal signal.
2. Approach and entrance; follow actual corridors rather than room-number order.
3. All 20 room cards are available, including hazards, inhabitants, checks, treasure and source references. Tactical sections connect to the exploration map.
4. Major situations include the smelter/flameskull, Mormesk’s bargaining, the spectator/Forge, Nezznar’s negotiations and tactics, Nundro’s rescue and changing responses from surviving occupants.
5. Review spell effects, saves, terrain, concentrations and resources through the existing combat workflow. The book expects level 4 or above; use the actual saved mixed-level party. Never silently level everyone or refill resources.
6. Rest, retreat and return use the saved expedition and Wave Echo journal. Flameskull recovery, inhabitants and temporary Forge effects require their recorded timing.
7. Homecoming, mine custody and Waterdeep transition remain branches based on actual outcomes; no ending is chosen in advance.
Stop: a secure refuge, retreat to town or a major resolved encounter. Wave Echo Cave can take several evenings.
Suggested pacing: 15–20 approach, 60–90 exploration, 45–60 major encounter, 15 retreat/closing.
