LOCAL SETUP
1. Revoke the API key previously pasted into chat and create a replacement in ElevenLabs Developers > API Keys. A local comparison confirmed the saved app key matches that disclosed key; it was not used for these plugin auditions. Allow speech generation and read access to voices, models, and your account/subscription. Keep usage-based billing disabled.
2. Double-click Set-Up-ElevenLabs.command in output/campaign/launchers. Paste the key into its hidden local prompt. Keep it out of chat and campaign files.
3. Return here and choose Refresh voices. Under Account & audition budget, choose Free account credits only or Creator included credits only. For Creator, enter explicit per-sample and total limits. Approve that allowance, enable auditions, then Save audition setup. This does not change provider billing settings.
4. Select Check account allowance, then Load stock voices. A saved key is not a verified connection; an account check is not a speech test.
5. Choose a character and stock-voice candidate, then Generate this audition. Only the original script is synthesized; no books, character records, campaign secrets or phone data accompany it.

PRIVATE LISTENING AND SELECTION
Connect headphones or another private output on the DM computer. Its default speaker output might be the table TV. Confirm private output before Preview on this computer. Audio is not automatically published to the TV or phones. Stop preview and the existing Mute all control stop playback.

Listen for clear names, warmth, restraint, distinctness and consistent emotion. After the full sample plays, Use this voice for [character] records your choice. A staged candidate is not a selection; mock samples cannot be selected.

Save voice profile preserves direction notes. Clear selected voice and Undo last voice edit support corrections. Selection retains provider, model, voice, exact script fingerprint and audition reference. Changing voice/model identity returns it to proposed. Blank campaigns do not acquire this cast automatically.

DELIVERY LIMIT
The initial plugin samples use eleven_multilingual_v2 and unchanged script text. The local app supports Multilingual v2 and Eleven v3, preserving the model with each take. Saved pacing, pronunciation and emotional notes are proposed guidance; this build does not apply a pronunciation dictionary or force those notes into the speech. Listen before treating any direction as achieved. Only stock voices are available; cloning is not included.

FOUR ORIGINAL AUDITIONS
These 35–50-word samples are hypothetical dialogue, not new campaign events or promises. Expanded direction and source references are in the app.

ELMAR BARTHEN
Elmar Barthen, at your service. Mind that coil of rope by the counter. Tell me what you need for the road, and we’ll work through it properly. Food first, then dry blankets. A bargain is no use if it leaves you cold and hungry.
Proposed direction: Warm, practical shopkeeper; about 145 words/minute. Welcome, concern, gentle exasperation, firm bargaining.
Proposed pronunciation: Elmar Barthen = EL-mar BAR-thun; Phandalin = fan-DAL-in.

SILDAR HALLWINTER
Sildar Hallwinter. Take a breath, and start with what you saw. Then tell me what you were told. We can act quickly without acting blindly. If someone is in danger, say so plainly. We will weigh the risks, and you will choose your course.
Proposed direction: Measured, humane authority; about 125 words/minute. Reassurance, urgency, disappointment, restrained relief.
Proposed pronunciation: Sildar Hallwinter = SIL-dar HALL-win-ter.

DRACUSETH MAW OF FLAMES
Call me Dracuseth. Sit, if you like. There is no need to turn every conversation into a council of war. Tell me what help you want, and what you would rather keep to yourselves. I can make an introduction. What comes after is your choice.
Proposed direction: Warm, plainspoken, patient; about 130 words/minute. Dry amusement, firm boundaries, protective concern; avoid constant growling.
Proposed pronunciation: Dracuseth = DRAK-yoo-seth; Waterdeep = WAW-ter-deep.
