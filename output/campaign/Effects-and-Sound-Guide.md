# Effects and soundscapes

Refresh the DM app and Player TV to load these controls. Use **Effects** in the top bar.

## Scene atmosphere

Choose **Off**, **Subtle**, or **Full**. This setting is saved with the campaign. Off stops optional animations and environmental soundscape layers; ordinary music and static spell outlines remain available. Device reduced-motion preferences also limit animation.

Weather, mist, dust, intensity, placed features, and sound layers are saved separately for each scene. Cave, camp, rain, snow, and storm presets are starting points you can edit.

Add a torch, fireplace, waterfall, mist, or dust feature. It starts as a **DM preview**. Use **Place on map**, then **Show feature on TV** when ready. Hidden/fogged features are excluded from player data. Decorative flames do not change dungeon vision: actual light is configured in **Dungeon**.

## Sound and narration

Choose **Sound & narration**, prepare a preset or add up to five recordings, then click **Play soundscape**. Each layer has its own volume; scene intensity adjusts the whole mix. The app uses existing recordings from the media library. It does not generate new music.

Once started, the soundscape follows scene changes with fades. It replaces the single ambience track, while music remains separate. Master volume, ambience volume, and mute apply to the layers. **Stop soundscape** stops just these layers. Import additional recordings through the existing media library.

**Begin narration** lowers music and ambience until **Finish narration**. Automatic scene-change ducking defaults to 12 seconds; set it to 0 to disable it. Scene transitions can be gentle or instant.

## Combat

HP changes and new conditions trigger a brief effect at the affected token. Resolved phone spells use their selected target tokens. In **Targets & spell zones**, you can also select a visible target and play an effect manually.

Create persistent areas with the existing combat area tools. Choose arcane, fire, ice, poison, or holy animation here. Public active areas animate until dismissed or expired using their configured combat duration/concentration rules. DM-only areas stay private.

## Labels

**Table labels** rotates map labels in quarter turns. Individual token names can follow that setting or have their own rotation toward the player seated nearby.

## Verification

Automated checks cover secret filtering, saved settings, expiry, target coordinates, repeated TV updates without repeated fades, effects off, UI controls, sound layer intensity, narration ducking, crossfade mute, and cancelled playback loads. Existing dungeon, travel, player tools, reconnect, run UI, Session 4 and table workflow checks also pass.

Physical TV appearance and speaker playback still need a brief check at the table. Start with Subtle, play a soundscape, test Mute, and rotate one token label toward its player.
