# Session 1 image guide

These are player-facing scene illustrations, not tactical maps. Every image is in the app's **Screen and map → What the players see** list for full-screen display, **Player handout → Image** list for display beside a map, and **Presentation → Media** library. The label in the table is the label to select in the app. The painted road, cave approach, and fogged Cragmaw Hideout maps are the tactical views; the app adds the optional 5-foot grid.

Open [Session-01-TV-Display.html](Session-01-TV-Display.html) for the main app. Keep the images in `session-01-visuals/` beside that file; the app uses those relative paths.

For area illustrations, keep **Cragmaw Hideout** selected in **What the players see** to preserve the fogged tactical map. Select the image under **Player handout → Image**, then click **Show on TV**. Hide the handout when the party leaves that area. Selecting an area image in **What the players see** instead fills the screen with that image; select **Cragmaw Hideout** again to return to the map.

The scene art is illustrative. Use the adventure map and actual positions for distances, cover, and movement. The two revealed-trap images show trap mechanisms, so do not show them during the initial walk down the trail.
