THE ECHO BELOW — MAP DELIVERY GUIDE
8 October 2026 · private campaign preparation

WHAT IS AVAILABLE
The atlas contains one world view, six regional detail plates, linked settlement
and site views, and twenty optional source-layout maps. All 472 canonical location
records are searchable. Only sourced anchors and clearly identified proposals
receive regional positions; unsupported venues and room positions remain unpinned.
The Nine stay private preparation. The Walking Tower is a roaming area; the Broken
Star marks a terrestrial approach to a partly planar destination, not a known portal.
Loudwater has no verified position on the supplied geographic reference and remains
unpinned. No map asset establishes a discovery, visit, defeat or ownership.

OPEN AND NAVIGATE
1. Open World → Campaign atlas.
2. Choose Expand atlas for a larger working view. Pan and zoom as needed.
3. Browse world → region → settlement/site → interior using the navigation links,
   or search the current view or whole atlas. The map register remains a separate
   read-only index under World → Map register.
4. Select a location to see its source, private notes, linked maps and Journey &
   discovery action. Existing shared travel records govern what the players know.
   Newly introduced atlas travel definitions start hidden; old discoveries remain.

SHOW A REVIEWED PLAYER VIEW
1. Choose Stage player view. Staging creates a private preview.
2. Inspect the preview: hidden places have no player records or pins; rumors have
   names only; exact pins require located/visited/cleared records. A hidden parent
   site also prevents exposing its interior board.
3. Choose Show staged view on TV. This shares the staged projection, not the DM
   atlas or its private source data. The party does not move and time does not pass.
4. Choose Return TV to live scene when finished. Atlas browsing and presentation
   leave the underlying live battle map and fog intact.
If knowledge changes after staging, stage the view again before showing it.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION FOR DUNGEON MAPS
Stage player view shares the ENTIRE ordinary unkeyed floor layout. Clean artwork
has no room keys, occupants, treasure or secret connectors, but unexplored geometry
can itself reveal information. For gradual exploration, select Prepare separate
battle map. That creates an opt-in variant with blank fog and its own scene identity.
No old tokens move; no old fog, drawings, notes, original images or scene IDs are
replaced. Reveal only the geometry reached in play using the new board's fog tools.
Review the source notes, scale and entry before use. Never copy old painted-map
coordinates onto the revised source geometry merely because grid counts look alike.

GEOGRAPHY AND SCALE LIMITS
The supplied official map and specific adventure pages were inspected; this was
not a page-by-page inspection of complete sourcebooks. Source notes distinguish
visual checks, text/OCR checks, manual transfers and custom proposals. Regional
lines and outlines are generalized, not exact coastlines, watershed boundaries or
surveyed roads. Generated painting contours can drift from those coordinates,
including the Neverwinter river mouth and Mount Hotenow peak. Source coordinates
were retained. Never use the painting to overrule published distances or source
room connections.
Only directly supported route distances are numeric. The five-mile ambush-to-hideout
trail and fifteen-mile Phandalin-to-Wave-Echo relation were visually checked in
Lost Mine of Phandelver. Other records identify their evidence or remain unknown.
No miles, days or physical table scale are derived from image pixels. River lines
do not establish boat passage; a proposed approach does not guarantee a safe route.
Tactical grids describe the authored source-coordinate reconstruction. Irregular
walls, rubble, roofs, foliage and furnishings remain simplified.

WATERDEEP AND FUTURE SETTLEMENTS
Waterdeep city uses the supplied SCAG printed56 city map as a 1095×1357 north-up
reference frame. Its new clean painting is 1127×1396 native raster pixels. The
castle and northern inner wall received a reviewed correction. Fine streets,
blocks and eastern countryside remain illustrative. Portal is an approximate
published ward anchor; Mending Court and custom businesses have proposed block
positions, not established addresses or lodging agreements.

Navigate Waterdeep → proposed neighborhood → Mending Court → shared area, available rooms or the permitted bindery. The 14 original Waterdeep SVG views also include Portal/well,
Second Margin, Ninepin Works, Lantern House, Blue Lantern Quay and three flexible
rooms. The collection's access requirement is preparation to confirm in play,
not recorded permission. Neutral player captions and opaque public IDs keep
private room purposes out of public projections. Private room/entrance labels
appear only on DM views and DM exports.

For gradual room exploration, Prepare separate battle map creates its own blank
fog. Never apply the original painted-map coordinates to a new layout. Existing
visual scene IDs, courtyard beats, leases, relationships, stored objects and
Undermountain state remain unchanged. Return links are navigation only.

Leilon is the most plausible prepared optional stop on the proposed southbound
High Road route. Its overview is a new 1536×1024 painting using a 1200×800 frame.
Five original service points start hidden; contact names, stock, prices, spells,
room availability and terms are unknown until the DM introduces them. A separate
editable SVG planning diagram is retained as an alternative arrangement. It is
not a published town plan and does not match the painting parcel for parcel.

Neverwinter, Luskan, Daggerford and Baldur's Gate have lighter vector overviews.
Neverwinter/Luskan/Baldur's Gate generalize selected supplied city maps.
Daggerford is a conceptual diagram based on published prose. Their decorative
roof blocks do not represent source-verified buildings or streets.

All new SVG artifacts are resolution-independent vectors. Waterdeep tactical

These are not native raster pixel claims. The neighborhood has no tactical grid;
other Waterdeep grids describe authored play-space dimensions. Only the stated

RUN A SETTLEMENT OR NEIGHBORHOOD STOP
Each primary settlement overview now has a collapsed DM-only Run this stop panel.
It exposes optional arrival framing, entry areas, broad districts, ordinary service
requests, existing contact references and onward route choices. Leilon includes a
short original arrival line and alternatives for continuing or addressing one
specific need. Unknown contact names, availability, costs and agreements remain
explicitly unset. Opening a card changes no money, inventory, time or travel state.

The Mending neighborhood has four private clickable service waypoints: book
repair/notebooks, meals/packed food, packing/delivery advice, and shared landing
copying. These use the existing residents and open the existing relevant maps.
They are preparation links, not new discovered places, residence bounds or rights
of access. The ordinary city-to-court introduction sequence still governs who has
actually met whom. Full private room entry requires its separate permission review.

A private proposed street line points toward the Yawning Portal off the map. Its
edge is a direction cue, not the inn's surveyed parcel or door. It has no distance
or compass claim. Players receive the clean ordinary streets; these service points,
contact notes and proposed direction line are excluded from public projections.
Existing venue People links use the original NPC IDs; missing or unintroduced
contacts are reference material, not automatically added as present characters.
