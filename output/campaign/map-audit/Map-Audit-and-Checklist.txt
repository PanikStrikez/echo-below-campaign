GEOGRAPHY AND IDENTITY RULES
Location-Register.json is the editable authority for identity, provenance and preparation. Map-Register-Data.js is generated from it. Approved outcome records and campaign saves retain authority over actual play and disclosure.
Existing scene, room, marker, route and site IDs are aliases/links, not renamed or migrated. A location may have several maps. A map may show several locations. Source books and original artwork remain unchanged.
Walking Tower has a roaming region, not a fixed pin. Broken Star has a regional approach and partly planar destination, not a new travel power. Character homes and the threatened district remain unplaced where choices are open.
Book-suggested Tales placements remain adaptations. Tamoachan in Chult and Doomvault in Thay belong in separate geographic views; Bloodgate Keep is a different location.
No location is discovered because its name or art exists. New atlas travel definitions start hidden; existing ledger states are preserved. Reading this register publishes nothing to TV or phones.

SCOPE AND LIMITATIONS
- Register combines the original static audit with completed atlas and source-layout additions. Browser-only uploaded/custom images and a newer unsaved live campaign were not independently inspected.
- Ready to reuse is limited to the stated purpose: geographic orientation or explicitly prepared new map variant. It does not certify exact source geometry, physical table calibration or flawless image registration.
- World and all six regional annotated sheets were rendered and visually reviewed. Generated terrain can drift from official reference coordinates: Neverwinter river-mouth and Mount Hotenow peak alignment are documented examples. Plates are not seamless tiles.
- Twenty Phandelver SVG variants received manual source-map and room-key comparison, full-render inspection and targeted topology/opening checks. Natural contours, clutter, vegetation and some furnishings are simplified. The 620 checks are regression evidence, not proof of pixel-perfect tracing.
- Book audit covers meaningful regions, settlements, venues and destinations; it was not a page-by-page inspection of every complete book. Unplaced minor venues, dungeon rooms, individual Ten-Towns settlements and Loudwater have no invented regional pins.
- Personal PDFs remain local read-only references. Scan offsets differ, particularly Dragon Heist; source references distinguish visual verification from text extraction and inference.
- The register, private atlas data, DM annotated SVGs, source scans and topology companion remain private. Public projection includes only reviewed artwork, approved broad geography and discovery-filtered records; Presentation blocks flagged composites.
- Existing scene IDs, selected original art and saved fog/tokens/notes are retained. A source variant is an opt-in new scene with blank fog, not an automatic migration. Staging an unkeyed map exposes its full ordinary layout.
- No miles or travel durations are calculated from image coordinates. Only directly sourced distances are numeric; other distances remain unknown. Walking Tower is a roaming area, Broken Star an approach area; proposed Nine placements do not establish discovery.
- Arrival-routing follow-up is tracked separately until its integration and preservation checks complete.
