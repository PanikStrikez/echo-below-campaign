Quiet phone waiting view — verified 14 October 2026

Where to find it
Reload the phone page. Home / My turn shows a compact Current objective card while
waiting, with the round, a permitted active-creature label and pending requests.
Tap a pending summary to deliberately open its relevant controls. Sheet, Explore,
Journal and Items keep their existing stable navigation.

Changes
Urgent tasks: saving throws, reactions, clarification, initiative when eligible,
your turn, failed-request correction and disconnection. A persistent compact
alert stays beside navigation. Only an explicit tap navigates to its controls.
New urgent tasks may vibrate once when the browser supports it; routine approval,
completion and repeated polling do not vibrate. Quiet updates are not live-announced.
Routine action requests are folded into Pending requests and Completed request
history. Combat replies are folded into Response history; closed requests retain
the last response received on this phone, without becoming urgent again. This
local response history is capped at 40 and clears on character reassignment.
It is presentation history, not canonical campaign history; it is not portable.
Success notices are grouped under Updates, with repeated identical notices counted.
Failures stay conspicuous and remain available in the same history.
Journal, Items and spell lists no longer redraw for unrelated turn/result updates.
Current tabs are not switched by new tasks, routine updates or a live disconnect.
Local quest browsing remains available offline; sending remains disabled.
Walkthrough wording now states: only your token can request movement; DM placement
is required; map input must be open; close an action draft to drag; taps in a draft
choose targets. Exploration and destination requests also require DM approval.
Quick rolls do not resolve actions or spend resources; prepared actions never send
automatically. Skipping onboarding stays dismissed for the current table join.
The actual joining flow now retains its join context through page reload so private
prepared drafts remain accessible after the QR code disappears from the address.

Verified
31 unit/mechanics tests passed, including 6 new attention-classification checks.
Existing composer/request, walkthrough and player-tools scripted regressions passed.
Waiting-view browser rehearsal checks tabs, retained DOM and scroll on Sheet,
Explore, Journal and Items; zero routine vibrations; combined urgent tasks and one
nudge per new task; private clarification excluded from another phone response;
explicit alert navigation; physical save and reaction responses; folded completed
history after closure; offline Journal/Completed browsing; reconnect; 360/390 widths;
actual joining/reload retaining private drafts and dismissed onboarding.
Urgent-banner computed contrast was measured and checked against 4.5:1. Visual
inspection found and corrected the shared hover style that had darkened its background.
Browser regression rehearsals also passed: private turn drafts (8 checks) and all
five action-card layouts (16 checks). All services and browser profiles were isolated.

Remaining checks
Real phone readability, actual vibration behavior and physical table use were not
tested. No claim of improved human task times is made. Response history preserves
only statuses received during this phone-page lifetime; current service action
history remains the authoritative request record. Missing active-creature names
remain generic and never reveal hidden NPCs.
No real campaign save, character statistics, map state, assets, source books,
phone assignments or live phone service were changed by the rehearsal.
No phone-service restart is required for this UI update.
