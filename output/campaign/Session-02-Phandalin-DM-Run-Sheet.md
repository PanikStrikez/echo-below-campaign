## Session aim

Let the players decide who to trust in Phandalin while pursuing Gundren. A successful session can end with **one** of these outcomes: a concrete town lead, a Redbrand confrontation, a decision to investigate Tresendar Manor, or a different lead that the party chooses. The town does not have to be cleared in one evening. The numbered town beats are a menu after the opening, not an order the players must follow.

## 1. Outside Barthen's - open the scene (10 minutes)

**Say:** "The road ends in a street of packed earth and old stone. A trading house stands ahead, its doorway busy with local customers. Sildar slows beside you and studies the town as if he is trying to remember the face of an old friend. Gundren should have been here before you. He isn't. What do you do?"

Ask who approaches, what happens to the wagon, and whether Sildar goes in with them. Do not make a player roll merely to enter the shop or ask about Gundren.

## 2. Barthen's Provisions (15-25 minutes)

**Elmar Barthen:** kind, practical proprietor; he expected the supplies and Gundren. His two clerks are Ander and Thistle. He knows Gundren has not come to town, mentions the Rockseeker brothers, and is upset by news of the abduction. He cannot point to Gundren's location. He knows the Redbrands are intimidating local businesses and frequent the Sleeping Giant. [Adventure p. 16](../../sources/pdfcoffee.com_lost-mine-of-phandelverpdf-pdf-free.pdf#page=16).

**Say when they enter:** "Warm lamplight falls over rope, trail food, tools, and stacks of ledgers. Behind the counter, a thin shopkeeper looks up. His welcoming smile falters as he counts the faces coming through the door. 'Where's Gundren?'"

**Run:** If the wagon and goods are actually delivered, Barthen pays the published **10 gp per character**. Record custody and payment then, not on arrival. If the wagon is missing or damaged, have him inspect what arrived and discuss it openly. He does not sell weapons or armor here. If the party asks about a safe bed or local news, he can direct them to Stonehill Inn. If they ask about trouble, he describes Redbrand pressure without forcing a fight.

**Ask:** "What do you tell Barthen about Gundren?" Then "What lead do you want to pursue first?" Give each character a chance to contribute. Pebble can ask about the tasty stone rumor; Barthen knows ordinary ore and prospectors, not the secret of the Echo. Keep Trista's token private unless she chooses otherwise.

## 3. Sildar and the town crossroads (10-20 minutes)

Sildar wants a place to recover and information on Iarno Albrek, a missing member of the Lords' Alliance. Stonehill Inn is a natural next stop; Townmaster's Hall becomes useful after he gets oriented. He can help the party ask questions, but his firsthand account from Session 1 gives **no confirmed destination for Gundren**. Do not retroactively tell the players they learned Cragmaw Castle or the Black Spider. If they ask him directly, answer with only what he could have witnessed at your table and establish the next investigative lead through play. [Adventure pp. 14, 18-19](../../sources/pdfcoffee.com_lost-mine-of-phandelverpdf-pdf-free.pdf#page=14).

**Say if Sildar speaks:** "I'll look for a room and ask after Iarno. Gundren needs us to keep looking. Let's gather something firmer than a rumor before we pick a road."

## 4. Stonehill Inn or free town exploration (30-60 minutes)

**Toblen Stonehill:** warm innkeeper who wants guests safe. **Trilena:** knows local families and is worried about the Redbrands. **Pip:** child who heard of a secret tunnel near Tresendar Manor through his friend Carp. **Other townsfolk:** miners and traders can corroborate Redbrand bullying. Do not pour out every lead at once; let the players choose whom to speak with. [Adventure pp. 15-16](../../sources/pdfcoffee.com_lost-mine-of-phandelverpdf-pdf-free.pdf#page=15).

**Say at the inn:** "The inn is warmer and louder than the street. Supper, wet cloaks, and low conversation crowd the common room. Several people glance toward your travel-worn gear. A child at one table stops talking mid-sentence."

**Offer one lead per conversation:**

| If they ask about... | A fair lead | Book |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gundren | Barthen has not seen him; the Rockseeker brothers have been away. The party still needs evidence of where he was taken. | p. 16 |
| The Redbrands | They bully businesses, spend time at the Sleeping Giant, and are connected to Tresendar Manor. | pp. 15-19 |
| Missing townsfolk | Trilena has heard the Dendrar family were taken after Thel opposed the gang. Treat their fate seriously and let players choose to act. | pp. 15-16 |
| A quiet route to the gang | Pip's friend Carp found a tunnel by Tresendar Manor; Qelline Alderleaf can introduce him. | pp. 16, 18 |
| Work outside town | Daran mentions Old Owl Well; Qelline points toward Reidoth and Thundertree; Sister Garaele has a delicate request; Harbin posts an orc reward. | pp. 16, 18 |

**Other useful locations:** Lionshield Coster if the party carried the cave's stolen marked goods; Linene Graywind can recognize them and reward their return. Halia Thornton at the Miner's Exchange wants the Redbrand leadership removed but has her own interests. Do not give those rewards until the goods or objective is actually presented. [Adventure pp. 16-17](../../sources/pdfcoffee.com_lost-mine-of-phandelverpdf-pdf-free.pdf#page=16).

**If they focus on finding Gundren:** Barthen and Sildar establish urgency, but neither supplies a destination. A captured street ruffian can confirm the Redbrands abducted Gundren, though ordinary members do not know where he was taken. Searching Iarno's desk finds a **custom Redbrand transfer record** naming Gundren and Cragmaw Castle; Iarno knows the same facts if questioned. Qelline can independently point them toward Reidoth in Thundertree as someone who knows the region. Halia can point them toward the Redbrands' goblin; Droop knows only a broad direction, while a captured bugbear can provide a more precise castle lead. Reveal each clue only when earned. If they choose an out-of-town lead, use the next location's book entry; Session 2's prepared TV combat map covers Phandalin and the hideout.

**Source boundary:** The Redbrand abduction and transfer record are this campaign's established addition. The party's Session 1 account remains that unidentified humanoid figures took Gundren. Keep the custom evidence separate from the adventure's printed Black Spider letter.

## 5. Redbrand pressure - only when earned (20-45 minutes)

The published chapter calls for **four Redbrand ruffians** to provoke a confrontation after the party has spent some time in town, or when they investigate the Sleeping Giant or Tresendar Manor. Let the players speak, leave, bargain, seek help, or fight. The ruffians are hostile and arrogant, not mindless. If three are defeated, the last attempts to flee toward Tresendar Manor. [Adventure p. 19](../../sources/pdfcoffee.com_lost-mine-of-phandelverpdf-pdf-free.pdf#page=19).

**Say when the pressure becomes direct:** "Four people in dirty red cloaks drift into your path. One plants a boot on the edge of the boardwalk. Another watches the townsfolk make room without being asked. The first says, 'Visitors usually learn how things work here before they start asking questions.' What do you do?"

**Combat card:** each ruffian AC **14**, HP **16**, speed **30 ft**, initiative **+2**. On its action it makes **two shortsword attacks**, each **+4 to hit, 1d6+2 piercing**. No automatic surprise. Four ruffians can make eight attacks per round; make their numbers and weapons visible before initiative so players can choose their response. Keep bystanders out of automatic damage. A captured ruffian can identify the gang as Gundren's abductors and point to the manor, but does not know his destination. [Adventure appendix B p. 61](../../sources/pdfcoffee.com_lost-mine-of-phandelverpdf-pdf-free.pdf#page=61).

**TV:** switch to the [player-safe street map](session-02-visuals/phandalin-street.svg) only when position matters. Use the app's grid, then place visible ruffians. Do not show the printed Phandalin map or a marked hideout map on the player TV.

Open [adventure pp. 20-21](../../sources/pdfcoffee.com_lost-mine-of-phandelverpdf-pdf-free.pdf#page=20) privately. Ask whether they use the ruined manor entrance (area 1) or the hillside tunnel (area 8). Switch the app to its unmarked [hideout TV map](session-02-visuals/redbrand-hideout.svg) with fog. The app's Session 2 runbook now has all twelve keyed areas, their original read-aloud prompts, checks, choices, and encounter plans. The marked book map remains on the DM screen. Reveal only explored rooms and deploy enemies only when encountered.
- **Straight after Gundren:** Acknowledge urgency. Barthen and Sildar cannot conjure a destination; allow tracking, questioning a Redbrand, or gathering corroborated leads. The party chooses a route once there is evidence. Do not announce the Redbrands or the castle before discovery.
- **Avoid the Redbrands:** Let them pursue town errands or leave. The gang's behavior can worsen later without forcing this fight on the first visit.
- **Return to Cragmaw Hideout:** Yeemik remains in charge after honoring the bargain and releasing Sildar. Treat any new conversation or conflict there as a consequence of that deal, not a reset of the cave.
- **Ask about the BELOW token:** Ask Trista whether she shows it. Donavan's observation gives him reason to ask her, not knowledge of the token's name or meaning. No town NPC solves the larger Echo mystery here.
