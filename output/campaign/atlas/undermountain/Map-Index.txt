UNDERMOUNTAIN MAP INDEX — PRIVATE DM PREPARATION

Where to open it
  World → Waterdeep → Yawning Portal → Undermountain → Dungeon Level in the
  app's World atlas. Review a map privately, then Prepare saved floor view.
  The prepared map joins the current expedition; it does not replace the
  existing `um1-table` canvas or its room records. Use Show on TV to stage it.

Player exploration
  level-1-player.svg is the clean, unkeyed full-floor source layout.
  It begins under dense manual fog in the saved scene. Focus one of the eight
  sections below to enlarge play; all sections retain the same tokens,
  objects, doors, fog and combat. Drag on the DM map to reveal, Shift-drag to
  conceal. The grid appears only at useful tactical zoom. The keyed source
  and undiscovered secret geometry stay private.

For private keys and source checks
  level-1-dm-keyed.svg; Level-1-Topology-DM.json;
  Level-1-Map-Audit.txt. The eight `-dm-keyed.svg` section crops share the
  same source frame as their `-player.svg` counterparts. Eleven secret
  geometry overlays are available through the map's DM review; enabling one
  never opens a door or qualifies the party for a gate automatically.

Return route and restrictions
  The well in area 1 returns to the Yawning Portal through the actual winch
  arrangement. Area 39a's ordinary stairs descend to Level 2 area 1a.
  Area 26d is a restricted gate toward Level 10, not the return route.
  Area 24a is a destroyed gate. Known gate locations and their activation
  requirements are separate private records in Floor-Workflow.json.

Later floors
  Floor-Workflow-Guide.txt and Floor-Workflow-Template.json provide the
  next-floor preparation process, verified route distinctions and conditional
  Level 2, Level 3 and Skullport source audits. Their complete room-by-room
  playable maps are not yet drawn or registered. Prepare the next reachable
  section when the party actually chooses that descent; browsing the atlas
  never records an arrival.

Scale and precision
  The supplied Level 1 map is a 1443 × 1900 raster with printed ten-foot
  squares. This independent SVG is a hand-traced, resolution-independent
  derivative aligned to that grid; it is not a publisher vector extraction.
  The saved scene uses a 300 × 200 five-foot subgrid. Irregular wall edges
  remain approximate. A full floor cannot provide one-inch physical squares
  on a normal table TV; zoom to a tactical section and calibrate that view.
