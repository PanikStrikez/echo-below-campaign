UNDERMOUNTAIN — PRIVATE SOURCE AUDIT AND LATER-FLOOR WORKFLOW

Scope
This is preparation, not a record of visits. Floor-Workflow.json is a private
reference with 23 connections, five campaign hook records and nine conditional
refuge candidates. Floor-Workflow-Template.json is an unregistered blank example.
Neither file changes the app, saved dungeon, discoveries, characters or contracts.

False shortcuts and actual exceptions
L 1/24a is a destroyed gate with residual wild magic, not transit. L 1/27 enters
a local demiplane with a return to the same alcove. L 1/4 vents require Tiny or
gaseous form. L 1/37's map is symbolic, not a usable floor plan or active portal.
General teleportation/planar escape is restricted (pp 10–12). Skullport 27 has a
specific exception: Tasselgryn alone can cast teleportation circle in her shop,
for 50 gp per person upfront, to her Waterdeep destination or an appropriate known
circle (p 308). No free or generally reversible city portal is implied.

The sea-cave route exists, but the hoist is broken (p 303). Small craft need an
actual haulage plan around the barrier; large ships cannot simply sail through.
The Skullport inn's secret 300 ft tunnel leads to Xanathar's hideout (p 308), not
to an assumed safe city exit. Its destination map needs separate preparation.

Refuges need conditions
L 1/22 shows traces of earlier rests, not guaranteed safety. L 2/11b is occupied
by Midna, who initially refuses sharing. The bazaar is a market, not free lodging.
Azrok's Hold requires registration, an audience/tribute and current permission.
Skullport's Guts&Garters charges 5 gp/day and has a spying proprietor. Keel Hall
and Dalagor's Fortress have faction conditions; no membership is granted here.
Tanor'thal Refuge becomes useful only after its spiders are actually cleared.

Reusable preparation loop
1. Read the approved saved position, resources, known passages and faction
   outcomes. Pick one reachable objective and a concrete return condition.
2. Read its source map and adjacent keys. Work from one coordinate master;
   derive crops from it. Keep doors, stairs, elevations and bottlenecks intact.
3. Record entry, exit, receiving area, reverse path and obstruction separately.
   Use null for unknown distance or unresolved junction. Read gate rules anew.
4. Put source keys, hazards, secret connections, hooks and NPC placement on a
   private layer. Keep custom hook placement separate from published geometry.
5. Review a clean public scene and its calibrated grid. An unkeyed whole-floor
   image still reveals the whole layout. Use limited scenes/fog where needed.
   Preserve old scenes, tokens and exploration; prepare a separate variant only
   through the explicit opt-in workflow.
6. Stage privately, then explicitly Show. Opening preparation does not travel,
   reveal, spend, spawn, award or reset. After play, record actual changes through
   the existing review and keep the same saved dungeon for the next expedition.

Conditional preparation boundaries
Level 2: first prepare the 1a arrival and whichever bazaar/western branch players
choose, with retreat. Baseline book party level 6. Select a plausible unsearched
home for the custom fragment only when needed; no default room is invented.
Level 3: first prepare catacombs arrival and the chosen route through/around the
drow territory. Baseline book party level 7. Prepare Azrok, river or foot-pass
choices from actual contact and resources, not a mandatory Skullport itinerary.
Skullport: prepare one arrival, one selected service/contact and a credible exit.
Keep lower/middle/upper town connected vertically rather than flattening them.
The other twenty floors remain references until a chosen route makes them relevant.

Open precision limits
No complete Level 2/3 tactical redraw or per-room door inventory was performed.
No new route lengths were inferred from illustration pixels. Current party
readiness, discovered route, living inhabitants and agreements were not inferred.
All new workflow material stays private until an explicit later integration.
