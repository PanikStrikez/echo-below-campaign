# The Echo Below — DM Questbook

DM-only • updated 9 October 2026 • 40 quests and 146 objectives

This book is generated from the app’s quest library. All objectives are planned opportunities, not completed events. Actual outcomes override planned resolutions. New staging details and names remain editable proposals.

## How to run it

1. Open **Session → Quest guide** (also in Story’s tool selector). Select the current act.
2. Choose from **Next leads**, or browse by category and pin a different quest. Only a few suggestions appear at once.
3. Open an objective to see the DM guidance. Record a result and source when it changes. Failed or bypassed objectives do not force a dead end.
4. Use **Change quest status / close quest** to record the actual outcome. Completing the last objective does not silently complete the quest or advance the act.
5. Write a player-safe title and current objective in **Player preview**. Publishing automatically routes the main quest to the TV, side quests to party phones, and character quests only to their owner. Act quests remain DM-only. No future chapter text is shared automatically.
6. **Show main quest on TV** updates its dedicated panel. **Hide main quest from TV** removes it immediately. The initial main objective uses only already party-known quest titles.
7. Progress edits withdraw the quest publication from its destination until reviewed again. The main TV panel updates directly; no duplicate text card is created. Older manually created TV text cards remain separately managed. Players may retain information already seen.
8. Quest Undo/Redo shares the continuity history (last ten changes). Campaign backups contain quest progress, notes, publication and recent history.

Private character objectives appear under the phone’s Journal & clues as a DM-updated quest. They are not automatically copied into the ordinary clue-sharing workflow. No phone-service restart is needed; refresh the DM app and phone pages.

Other campaigns must explicitly enable this campaign-specific guide.

## Story rules

- One main campaign investigation; one selected act; optional side quests; personal arcs with player-owned choices.
- Suggested prerequisites guide preparation, not access. Declining a side trip never removes the sole path to essential information.
- Record facts, rumors and planned scenes separately. No automatic rewards, map reveals, level increases, NPC knowledge or character choices.
- Quests can finish through negotiation, retreat, partial success or another credible outcome. Record what changed and what remains open.
- Actual outcome records created when closing a quest are private. Review and share appropriate wording through continuity if it should enter public recaps or linked map records.
- Use the runbook, NPC, site and dungeon buttons to prepare supporting content. Check published maps and room keys before placing custom hooks.

## Campaign rhythm

Before play: select an act, pin two or three useful leads, choose two character opportunities, and review what is published.
During play: follow their destination, use the keyed adventure, and record evidence and outcomes.
After play: review unresolved promises, rewards and custody in continuity; record quest outcomes; republish only current known objectives; choose a few causally justified faction and rival developments.
