VOICE STUDIO & SCENE NARRATION — 8 October 2026

FIND IT
Library > Voice studio. NPC cards also have Voice studio.
Runbook openings have Prepare narration. Published journal entries and current
recaps have Prepare spoken entry/recap. Shared, approved correspondence has
Prepare spoken letter. Handouts have Prepare narration. The studio source picker
collects these audience-approved selections in one place.

AT THE TABLE
1. Write a line or choose the exact published source text.
2. Select a stock voice, delivery, and optional pronunciation corrections.
3. Save. Expand Exact text sent for speech to review the actual spoken wording.
4. Generate, or import audio made for the saved script/settings.
5. Choose separate private headphones, preview the entire take, then approve it.
6. Play approved voice to table. The floating transport has Pause, Resume, Skip,
   and an immediate Stop. Mute all stops narration too.

Narration alone leaves the map and combat alone. Staging a visual and ambience
has its own explicit Show staged visual & ambience action. Queueing is optional;
Skip advances to the next queued approved recording. A finished recording does
not automatically start another one.

Use Approve text for table, then Subtitles only / read aloud myself if you prefer
manual narration. Captions show the full selected passage, not timed word-by-word
subtitles. Stop voice & subtitles clears them from the TV.

PRIVATE PREVIEW
Keep the computer default output on the table speakers. Choose named, physically
separate headphones for previews. The app checks output identities and refuses
private playback if separation cannot be established. A device change stops
playback and clears output choices. Check your physical sound routing before play.

Some browsers need microphone permission to expose output names. The alternate
output picker explains this before requesting access; any microphone stream is
immediately stopped, without recording or sending it. Unsupported routing leaves
previews blocked. Manual narration and approved subtitles remain available.

LOCAL FILES & CHANGES
Every take retains the exact script, NPC identity, voice/model, delivery settings,
pronunciation corrections, source fingerprint, file hash, and approval history.
Editing does not delete an earlier approved take; it makes that recording
outdated until you restore its matching script/settings or approve a new take.
Changes to approved recap events invalidate linked recap narration.

Recordings are stored locally in this browser. Use the existing full campaign
backup: it embeds all local takes, including older takes. Restore as a new campaign
on another browser. Provider keys, billing settings and sound-device choices are
not included. Select outputs again after restoring. Do not clear browser storage
before exporting a backup. Replay of saved takes makes no provider request.
