## Private history for the DM

Aldric served the Crown as a battle mage. His discipline in a great war brought him honors and the office of Crown Magistrate. Believing in law and duty, he obeyed orders to burn innocent villages and carry out mass executions because refusal threatened his wife and child. Guilt led him to work quietly against the Crown. When he was discovered, he returned home to find his house burned, his family killed, and his title given to another. Crown officials imprisoned and tortured him without trial, then killed him and disposed of his body.

He returned from death through a force no one has yet identified. His memories are fragmentary: grief without names, dread at smoke, and love for faces he cannot picture. He wears the magistrate's signet ring, scorched nearly smooth and trapped on a crooked finger. An unfamiliar language came to him when he woke. He wants both to atone for those he harmed and to learn his family's names, faces, and fate.

These are Bowman's character facts. The Crown's identity, his family names, and much of the Crown’s history remain open. The DM has now chosen a connection between Halaster and his return; the prepared experiment and evidence path are in [Five character stories](../campaign/Five-Character-Stories.md). These are unrevealed custom campaign material. Do not turn the mystery into a compulsory quest or reveal Aldric's crimes to the party for Bowman.

## Playing Aldric

- **Voice and manner:** Calm, precise words in a ruined rasp. He rarely raises his voice.
- **Triggers:** Smoke, gathering crowds, and children's laughter can make him go cold and silent. Let Bowman decide his response in each scene.
- **Ideal:** Atonement for acts he knows were unforgivable, even where memory is missing.
- **Bond:** The family he can feel but cannot picture; he wants their names and faces and the truth about who took them.
- **Flaw:** Under pressure, his old battle mage instincts reach for overwhelming force before his present conscience catches up.
- **Appearance:** Gray, bloodless skin; cold hands; burns from torso to jaw; crooked healed fingers; a traitor's brand; a hood that he lowers only when he chooses.
- **Languages:** Common, Dwarvish, Undercommon, Infernal. Confirm how his live sheet grants them before using language-based mechanics.

## Hooks to offer, not impose

1. A legal seal, title, or bureaucratic process in Phandalin stirs recognition without immediately naming the Crown.
2. A person harmed by another authority asks for help. Aldric decides whether aid means testimony, protection, restraint, or force.
3. Waterdeep records could someday hold a trace of the successor who took his magistrate's office. Provide leads only after Bowman shows interest.
4. The ring and the unfamiliar tongue may produce clues to his return. Keep the source of his resurrection separate from the Echo unless later play and Bowman support that connection.

**At-will:** Fire Bolt (+5, 1d10 fire), Ray of Frost (+5, 1d8 cold and speed penalty), Mage Hand. Dagger +3 to hit, 1d4+1 piercing, thrown 20/60 feet.

**Spellbook, six level-1 spells:** Burning Hands, Magic Missile, Mage Armor, Shield, Detect Magic, Find Familiar. The first four are prepared. Detect Magic and Find Familiar can be cast as rituals from the book without preparation; Find Familiar still needs its material component. Aldric has two 1st-level slots. Arcane Recovery restores one expended 1st-level slot once per day after a short rest.

**Reborn:** Medium; darkvision 60 feet; poison resistance; advantage on death saves and saves against disease or being poisoned; no need to eat, drink, breathe, or sleep; a 4-hour long rest. Knowledge from a Past Life adds 1d6 to a check using a skill, twice per long rest. The two extra skills from Ancestral Legacy are Insight and Perception. Common and Dwarvish reflect his prior life; Undercommon and Infernal are his Haunted One languages. His unexplained knowledge of one language remains a story clue rather than an extra mechanical proficiency.

**Background and gear:** Haunted One provides Investigation and Religion, Heart of Darkness, a monster hunter's pack, common clothes, a special trinket (the signet ring), and 1 sp. Wizard starting choices are dagger, arcane focus, scholar's pack, and spellbook. His worn ring has no magic bonus. The pack includes ordinary supplies; record consumption during play.

**Subclass timing:** Under the 2014 Wizard rules, Aldric chooses Evocation at level 2. He has no Sculpt Spells at level 1. The campaign's 2014 base uses Reborn from *Van Richten's Guide to Ravenloft* and the legacy Haunted One background as named exceptions.
