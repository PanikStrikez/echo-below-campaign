How to use this every session
  1. Choose a saved session or a session pack from the Session menu at the top. New sessions carry forward the party’s current HP, AC, and saved attacks.
  2. Add a map or illustration, then add encounters and combatants for this session.
  3. Add DM beats with your read-aloud text and private reminders.
  4. Open the player screen on the table TV and run the session.
  5. Use Encounters & combat to deploy waves, review rolls, resolve timed effects, and apply damage after checking targets.
  6. Use Back up campaign to save all sessions and uploaded images in one file.
