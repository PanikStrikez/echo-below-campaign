What changed
Consistent rows: name, timing, d20/save math, effect, named resource cost,
remaining resources and explicit unknown/situational/manual-ruling states.
Favorites appear once rather than duplicated in their ordinary group.
Full effects, source references, requirements and quick rolls are expandable.
Quick rolls explicitly send dice only; reviewed requests retain the same canonical
indices, targets, costs and existing DM approval workflow.
Depleted resources stay visible and can be raised for DM review; no availability
badge claims range, reaction triggers or every situational requirement was met.
Unprepared spells remain visible with request disabled. Unknown pools and costs
remain explicit. Shadow Arts notes declaring Focus costs are not presented as
spell-slot casting. Unarmed strike and Flurry retain distinct mechanics.
Wizard and cleric copies of Detect Magic remain separate, labeled choices.
Freeform intent is a derived owner-view entry: it does not enter the saved sheet,
grant an ability, consume an action/resource or resolve an effect automatically.
Its DM review requires a ruling. Offline requests disable; reconnect restores them.
