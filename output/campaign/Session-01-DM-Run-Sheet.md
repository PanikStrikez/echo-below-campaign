## Five-minute launch

1. Open [the campaign table](Session-01-TV-Display.html) on your computer. Choose **Session 1**, **Prep**, then the **Before play** beat. Leave the rules and source shelf on your DM display.
2. Click **Open player TV**, move that window to the flat table TV, and make it full screen. Check that the TV shows the campaign cover and that the DM screen reports it connected. Use the DM preview before revealing a new scene.
3. Open **Director**. Session 1 now has one-click cues for Gundren's departure, the dead horses, the road battle, the cave entrance, and the fogged hideout. Test the journey music quietly, then mute or stop it until play starts. The later Echo and Nine cues contain spoilers.
4. Use **Restore points → Create restore point now**, then **Back up campaign**. Check the road and cave images are available offline. The hideout map on both screens is an unlabeled, player-safe tactical interpretation; open the published room map privately when exact geometry matters.
5. Pebble’s handout supplies a provisional Fighter 1 card and token; confirm his current HP and Second Wind. Aldric’s DM-built level-1 sheet and token are ready: HP 8, AC 11 before Mage Armor, two spell slots, spell attack +5, save DC 13. Review his prepared spells with Bowman before play. Confirm all five characters’ current HP, light, and marching order. Ask Donavan which 1st-level spells are prepared and Ringgo for his second Expertise choice.

**Ready to begin:** The player TV shows only the cover; four goblins are unplaced; the cave is still fogged; the DM has a working TV connection and a restore point.

End at a natural choice or discovery; the hideout does not have to be cleared tonight. The Cragmaw continuation pack is ready for the next session.

## Pressure checks during play

- **Ambush is easy:** let the party win. The rescue lead is the next challenge.
- **A PC falls:** track death saves and make healing, stabilization, cover, or retreat options visible. There is no Dracuseth healing pool in this party.
- **Several PCs fall:** when consistent with enemy motives, show a credible retreat, surrender, or capture route. Preserve consequences and the dice.
- **Resources run low:** a defensible short rest is possible if the group secures a location; time and noise can matter.
- **After resolving the hideout:** consider the level-2 milestone for the level-1 PCs before further Phandalin threats. Confirm the new characters’ levels before applying advancement.
